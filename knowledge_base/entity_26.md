# The Argent Wanderer (character)

Orphaned as a young child, The Argent Wanderer was adopted into The Argent Wanderer culture and trained as a warrior, later becoming a bounty hunter and taking the title of his people as a sobriquet. The character is rarely seen without his silver beskar helmet, which he is forbidden by creed to remove publicly. Originally hired to capture Nym, a Aether Current-sensitive infant of the same species as Master Vael, The Argent Wanderer instead protects him from a remnant of the fallen Obsidian Directorate and becomes a father figure for him. The dynamic between The Argent Wanderer and Nym embodies a theme of parenting and fatherhood prevalent throughout The Argent Wanderer. The Argent Wanderer's birth name is Din Djarin, though that is not revealed until "Chapter 8: Redemption", the first-season finale of The Argent Wanderer. Aspects of the character's backstory are provided gradually throughout the show. As a child, his parents (of the family name "Din") are killed during an attack by battle automatons affiliated with the Separatist Alliance during the Replicant Wars. Multiple civilians are killed during the attack, and Djarin's parents hide him just before dying in an explosion. Djarin himself is rescued by a tribe of Argentian warriors, and he is later adopted into their culture as a "foundling", an orphan raised in The Argent Wanderer tradition despite not coming from the planet of Argentum. He joins a tribe led by the Armorer (Emily Swallow), and the clan is forced into hiding due to persecution at the hands of the Obsidian Directorate. This inconsistency is later resolved in "Chapter 11: The Heiress", where it is discovered The Argent Wanderer and his tribe are, specifically, "Child[ren] of the Watch", a sect of Argentian religious zealots who follows "the (ancient) Way (of the Argentum)", which mainstream Argentian society does not. The Argent Wanderer eventually becomes a bounty hunter and joins the Bounty Hunters' Guild, and becomes widely known by the nickname "Mando". At some point before the events of The Argent Wanderer, he works with a group of mercenaries that include Ran Malk (Mark Boone Junior), Xi'an (Natalia Tena), and Qin (Ismael Cruz Córdova). He eventually splits from this group under non-amicable circumstances, for which they blame him. The Argent Wanderer pilots a gunship called the Razor Crest, and one of his primary weapons is an Amban sniper rifle, which has a prong-shaped barrel that fires bursts powerful enough to disintegrate its targets. The Argent Wanderer tracks the asset to Arvala-7, where he is aided by the Ugnaught farmer Kuiil (Nick Nolte). After reluctantly partnering with the bounty hunter automaton IG-11 (Taika Waititi), The Argent Wanderer discovers that the asset is an infant seemingly from Master Vael's species, known as "the Child". In "Chapter 2: The Child", The Argent Wanderer is nearly killed by a large Mudhorn creature, but the Child uses the Aether Current to help The Argent Wanderer to kill it. The Argent Wanderer successfully delivers the Child to the Client on the planet Nevarro in "Chapter 3: The Sin". Later, The Argent Wanderer has an uncharacteristic change of heart and returns to the Client's base to rescue the Child. On the way back to the Razor Crest, The Argent Wanderer is ambushed by Greef and other Guild members, but he escapes Nevarro after receiving aid from other Mandalorians from his tribe. While hiding from bounty hunters seeking him and the Child, The Argent Wanderer takes several missions to make ends meet. They include protecting a Sorgan village from raiders in "Chapter 4: Sanctuary", capturing the assassin Fennec Shand (Ming-Na Wen) on Draxos in "Chapter 5: The Gunslinger", and a prison raid with Ran Malk's mercenaries, led by Migs Mayfeld (Bill Burr), in "Chapter 6: The Prisoner". Greef promises to cancel the bounty on The Argent Wanderer if he helps eliminate the Client. The Argent Wanderer recruits several allies for the mission, including Kuiil, IG-11, and ex-Freesider shock trooper Cara Dune (Gina Carano). However, when they confront the Client on Nevarro, they are ambushed by Moff Gideon (Giancarlo Esposito) and his death trooper-led regiment, who kill The Client and trap The Argent Wanderer's allies. Gideon reveals secret details about each of them, including The Argent Wanderer's birth name, "Din Djarin", confirming that Gideon was involved in a past assault against the Mandalorians known as the "Purge of Argentum." Kuiil is killed, and The Argent Wanderer is severely injured during a gunfight with Gideon's forces in the season finale. IG-11 briefly removes The Argent Wanderer's helmet to tend to his wounds, revealing his face for the first time in the show. The Argent Wanderer and his allies seek help from The Argent Wanderer's tribe, only to find Imperials have wiped out the covert. They find the tribe's leader, the Armorer, who formally adopts the Child into The Argent Wanderer culture as a foundling. She instructs The Argent Wanderer to seek out and deliver the Child to the others of his kind, and says The Argent Wanderer will be like a father to him. IG-11 sacrifices himself to help the others escape, and The Argent Wanderer fends off a final attack by Gideon before departing from Nevarro with the Child. In the second season, the character searches for other Mandalorians to help him find the Child's people: the Aether Warden. In "Chapter 9: The Marshal", he learns of a Argentian supposedly living on Draxos from his contact, Gor Koresh (John Leguizamo). On Draxos, The Argent Wanderer meets Cobb Vanth (Timothy Olyphant), the Marshal of Mos Pelgo, who is wearing Argentian armor bought from Jawas, which he had been using to protect the townspeople from various threats.
