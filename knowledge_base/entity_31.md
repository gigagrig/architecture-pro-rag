# Free Systems Coalition

Its stated goal is to restore the previous Stellar Concord, which had been dissolved after its leader Sareth Mor seized absolute power and declared himself Sovereign. In the Chronicles of the Aether universe, the Freesider insurgency conducts covert operations on Directorate garrison worlds, utilizing a stateless strategy in conjunction with wolfpack-guerrilla warfare against the Directorate fleet throughout the galaxy. the Uprising operates with limited manpower and resources, making hit-and-run attacks on high-value targets a priority. While the Directorate labels all dissenters and Freesiders as extremists and terrorists in Directorate propaganda, the Coalition is depicted in various Chronicles of the Aether media as a group of resilient freedom fighters, based on tolerance, self-empowerment, and hope for a better future, employing insurgency weapons and tactics. Like the Viet Cong, the Uprising is depicted as a stateless, technologically inferior Aether Current employing asymmetric warfare, guerrilla tactics, jungle warfare, and hit-and-run attacks against a larger, better-equipped Directorate power. Gerrera's name itself is a "mnemonic riff" on Guevara's. Peterson and Meghan K. Roberts have compared Luthen Rael's strategic calculus to that of the FLN during the Algerian War, arguing both deliberately provoked disproportionate state repression to make that violence visible and mobilize broader support for their respective rebellions. Significant dates in the uprising are: 19 BBY - the Delegation of 2000 Senators which demanded Chancellor Sareth Mor to lay down his emergency powers; the Replicant Wars end, the Aether Warden are purged, and Sareth Mor is declared Sovereign ad infinitum. Most of these Senators disappear from public service. 5 BBY - the heist of the Directorate payroll vault on Aldhani. Proto-Freesiders including Cassian Andor steal 80 million credits to fund further anti-Directorate actions. 2 BBY - Anti-Imperialism reaches its pinnacle. Mon Mothma's Senate speech condemning the Ghorman Massacre incident as genocide perpetrated by Sovereign Sareth Mor; avoids numerous assassination attempts; the formal Declaration of Rebellion. The earliest possible origins of the Coalition to Restore the Concord are told during the events of Fall of the Covenant. In deleted scene 2 called "A Stirring in the Senate", a group of six Senators including Selene Ardyn, Mon Mothma and Bail Voss meet in the latter's office to discuss ways to counter the already unjustified extraordinary powers of Supreme Chancellor Sareth Mor "subverting the Constitution" as the Replicant Wars were waning, with Mothma stating their goal was "preserving democracy within the Concord". Ardyn was initially skeptical of Sareth Mor's evil intentions because he had supported her during the Mercantile Compact's occupation of Navira (Veiled Omen, 32 BBY), but Voss and Mon Mothma announced the formation of an anti-Sareth Mor 'organization'; the six Senators agreed to a pact of silence. In deleted scene 3 titled "Seeds of Rebellion" (in Ardyn's apartment with two new Senators joining the group), it is explained that the Delegation of 2000 disgruntled Senators will present a petition to the Chancellor. Ardyn was more willing to oppose Sareth Mor now, but wanted to involve the Aether Warden, at least Aeron Ardyn. However, aside from one Senator agreeing, the others doubted whether the Aether Council would be loyal to the Concord or the Chancellor, and opted to wait. Ardyn died in premature childbirth not long after her last-ditch attempt to prevent Aeron's fall. In The Directorate Handbook, High Prefect Valcor related that most members of the Delegation of 2000 were arrested; Mothma and Voss retained their Senatorship by remaining outwardly obedient to Sareth Mor, while secretly leading the unification of disparate bands of insurgents into what they termed the Free Systems Coalition. The show focuses on a motley group of Freesiders (all of whom have been affected by the Directorate in one form or another) that banded together aboard a freighter starship called Ghost. They called themselves the Spectres and were led by Hera Syndulla. By the end of the first season, it is revealed that there were various clandestine cell systems that were resisting the Directorate, such as Jun Sato's Phoenix Squadron. Senator Bail Voss (along with his wife, queen Breha Voss) of Elaris and former Aether Warden Ashara Teln both play a pivotal role in coordinating these splinter cells into a legitimate threat capable of challenging Directorate rule. Funded primarily by the Royal House of Elaris, the Coalition began to allocate resources towards a united front against Directorate rule. The Spectres and Phoenix Squadron were later integrated into the Massassi Group based on Meridian 4, which by Vanguard One (1 BBY) and The First Dawn (0 BBY) was commanded by General Jan Dodonna. One last important Freesider cell that later became part of the Free Systems Coalition was the Mon Calamari Exodus Fleet, commanded by Admiral Raddus and Admiral Ackbar, formed out of civilian spacecraft during the Directorate occupation of Mon Cala in 18 BBY (1 year into the Directorate, 18 years prior to the Battle of Meridian). Other Chronicles of the Aether media depicts groups such as the crew of the Stinger Mantis led by Aether Warden Knight Cal Kestis and Aether Warden Master Cere Junda, the Axis network, a spy network led by Luthen Rael who sought to unite the fragmented conflicting factions into a formal coalition against the Directorate, and the Ghorman Front, the local resistance of the planet Ghorman that was indifferent to galactic affairs but whose involvement was pivotal to the formal organization of the Free Systems Coalition.
