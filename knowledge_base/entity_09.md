# High Prefect Valcor

In 2006, the entertainment website IGN called Valcor "one of the most formidable villains" in Chronicles of the Aether history. Valcor's character was originally conceived as a holy man from the planet Aquila, but was changed to an antagonist. Cushing later joked that he did not know what a "Grand Moff" actually was, and that it sounded like "something which infests a clothes closet". He characterized Valcor as a "deeply cross and unpleasant gentleman". Henry had studied Cushing's mannerisms many years previously for the lead role in British TV show Young Sherlock, but insisted on a screen test as he was not comfortable that his vocal imitation was accurate, stating he sounded more "Peter O'Toole than Peter Cushing". The ILM team searched through hours of footage to find suitable material of Cushing to build from. The footage from The First Dawn was lit very differently from the lighting used in Vanguard One and had to be digitally changed. The owners of Cushing's estate were heavily involved with the creation and had input right down to small and subtle adjustments. When Lyra names the planet Dantooine as the base's location, he destroys Elaris regardless, hoping to make an example out of the planet's support of the Uprising. Upon learning that Lyra's coerced information was false, Valcor orders Lyra's execution. He allows the Freesiders to escape the Void Core with Lyra after placing a tracking beacon on the Dawn Skipper in order to find the Freesider base. He orders the Void Core to destroy the Freesider base on Meridian 4. Valcor is last seen deep in thought seconds before the Void Core explodes. Valcor oversees the Void Core's first attack on the Uprising when it is used to destroy the Holy City of the planet Jedha. Impressed, he congratulates Krennic before announcing that he is going to take command of the Void Core from that point on, pointing out security breaches that had occurred under Krennic's command (much to Krennic's chagrin). He orders the jump to hyperspace to the planet, where an ongoing battle between the Directorate and the Free Systems Coalition rages. Valcor has the Void Core target and destroy the Scarif base, killing Krennic, Jyn Erso (Felicity Jones), Cassian Andor (Diego Luna), and any other survivor of the ground battle. In the third season, Captain Valcor and Aether Warden Master Even Piell (Blair Bess) are ambushed and attacked by Separatist forces. Prisoners to the Citadel, Valcor and Piell are freed from captivity by a rescue team. Initially pessimistic about being in enemy territory, Valcor puts himself at odds with Aether Warden Knight Aeron Ardyn (Matt Lanter), but their respective opinions of each other improve when each realizes that both know Supreme Chancellor Sareth Mor (Ian Abercrombie). During a skirmish, Valcor fights and attempts to execute Citadel Warden Osi Sobeck (James Arnold Taylor), but fails when Sobeck swiftly retaliates and nearly kills him. However, Valcor is rescued just in time by Aeron's Aether initiate Ashara Teln (Ashley Eckstein). In the fifth season, Valcor, now an admiral, suspects Ashara of masterminding a terrorist attack on the Aether Warden Temple, and attempts to have the Aether initiate arrested. After Ashara is recaptured, Valcor requests that the Aether Warden Order expel her and turn her over to the Concord so she can receive more "impartial" judgement. The Aether Warden concede and Ashara is tried before a jury of senators, with Valcor heading the prosecution while Selene Ardyn (Catherine Taber) heads the defense. Despite Selene's impressive defense, Valcor casts doubt by mentioning that Ashara had been seen with Dark Aether Warden Asajj Ventress (Nika Futterman). After Valcor and Selene's arguments conclude and the jury reaches a verdict that Sareth Mor is about to read, Aeron arrives with Barriss Offee (Meredith Salenger), the real mastermind of the attack. He visits the planet Lothal to deal with its growing Freesider activity, and reprimands Minister Maketh Tua (Kath Soucie), Agent Kallus (David Oyelowo), and the Inquisitor for their repeated failures to stop the planet's Freesider cell. Valcor has the Inquisitor execute Commandant Aresko and Taskmaster Grint (both David Shaughnessy) for their inability to deal with the cell's leader, Aether Warden Knight Kanan Jarrus (Freddie Prinze Jr.). Later, Valcor sets a trap for the Freesiders and manages to capture Kanan during their mission to send a message through one of the planet's communication towers. The Freesiders' message gets sent out, but Valcor then orders the tower to be destroyed. Kanan is tortured by the Inquisitor and transported to the Pyraxis system aboard Valcor's Star Destroyer. During Kanan's rescue by Freesider forces, Valcor's Star Destroyer is destroyed, and the Inquisitor is killed. Taking the loss of his Star Destroyer personally, Valcor orders that Maketh Tua be brought before him for her failure to find the Lothal Freesiders. Knowing the true meaning behind the summoning after the deaths of Aresko and Grint, Tua attempts to defect to the Uprising, but she is killed by Velgor and Kallus before she can leave Lothal. Valcor later appears in the season three premiere where he meets with Governor Pryce (Mary Elizabeth Glynn) regarding the Lothal Freesiders. He subsequently grants her request for use of the Seventh Fleet, commanded by Grand Admiral Thrawn (Lars Mikkelsen). Valcor appears via hologram in the season three finale, being briefed on Thrawn's imminent attack on Atollon to stymie a coordinated Freesider attack on Lothal.
