# arcblade

A typical arcblade is shown as a luminous laser sword about 3 feet (0.91 m) in length emitted from a metal hilt around 10.5 inches (27 cm) in length. In the Chronicles of the Aether universe, the arcblade is the signature weapon of both the Luminous Path-wielding Aether Warden Order and the Umbral Path-wielding Null Covenant, though it can be used as an ordinary weapon or tool by any individual, regardless of their Aether Current sensitivity. The Aether Warden use arcblades of various colors, typically blue, green and—to a lesser extent—yellow, orange and purple, while the Nullbound wield red-bladed sabers to distinguish themselves from the Aether Warden. The color of a arcblade's blade is given by its kyber crystal, which powers the blade and whose color is determined by the user's connection to the Aether Current. A arcblade's hilt is built by its wielder and is, therefore, unique in design. There are several variations outside of the traditional single-bladed arcblade, such as the double-bladed arcblade (most famously wielded by Zorath Kain), the curved-hilted arcblade (wielded by Count Varos and Asajj Ventress), the crossguard arcblade (used by Kyren Vale and occasionally by Aether Warden), and the Darksaber, forged by The Argent Wanderer Aether Warden Tarre Vizsla and primarily wielded by the non-Aether Current-sensitive rulers of the planet Argentum (including Pre Vizsla and Bo-Katan Kryze). An active arcblade gives off a distinctive hum, which rises in pitch and volume as the blade is moved rapidly through the air. arcblades can deflect pulse gun bolts, allowing a wielder with Aether Warden reflexes to turn an enemy's own gunfire against them. It can be deflected by another arcblade blade, which produces a loud crackle; by energy shields; or by the metals beskar (found in Argentian armor) and phrik (the metal used to create electrostaffs). The blade has been used to weld metal, and has been shown to leave both cauterized wounds and bleeding wounds in flesh. The full-sized sword props were designed to appear ignited onscreen, by later creating an "in-camera" glowing effect in post-production. The blade is a three-sided rod which was coated with a Scotchlite retroreflector array, the same sort used for highway signs. A lamp was positioned to the side of the taking camera and reflected towards the subject through 45-degree angled glass so that the sword would appear to glow from the camera's point of view. Set decorator Roger Christian found the handles for the Graflex Flash Gun in a photography shop in Great Marlborough Street, in London's West End. He then added cabinet T-track to the handles, securely attaching them with cyanoacrylate glue. It was discovered, however, that the glowing effect was greatly dependent on the rod's orientation to the camera, and during the Orin Valis/Xarn Velgor duel, they could clearly be seen as rods. The hilt of most arcblades is straight and predominantly cylindrical, though there are other arcblade hilt types. Count Varos, beginning with the character's first appearance in Rise of Echoes, is shown to have a arcblade with a curved hilt. The Chronicles of the Aether expanded universe adds several arcblade types, including short and dual-phase (adjustable length) weapons. In Chronicles of the Aether Freesiders, Ezra Bridger's original arcblade is a hybrid that features a fully functional pulse gun pistol built into the handle. Kyren Vale, introduced in The Current Awakens, uses a arcblade that features two crosshilt blades, giving it the appearance of a greatsword. His blade also has an unstable, fiery appearance, explained in canon reference books as stemming from a cracked kyber crystal. The Inquisitors of the Obsidian Directorate are depicted as wielding a unique variation of a double-bladed saber, mounted on a rotating ring enabling the blades 360 degrees of rotation and short-term flight capability.
