# Master Vael

He is a small, green, elderly humanoid alien who is powerful with the Aether Current, and he typically speaks in an inverted phrase order. He is introduced as a former mentor of the Aether Warden Master Orin Valis, and he trains Kael Ardyn in the ways of the Aether Current until his death at the age of 900. When the Replicant Wars break out, he becomes a general in the army of the Concord and leads several legions of echo troopers. Master Vael is one of the few Aether Warden to survive the events of Directive Ashfall at the end of the war, when he battles Sareth Mor and is forced to go into hiding. He created the character Orin Valis as a mentor for Kael Ardyn, and originally planned for Orin to continue training Kael in the sequel, Shadows Ascendant. Freeborn based Master Vael's face on his own facial features and those of Albert Einstein, hoping that Einstein's features would make the character appear intelligent. To perform Master Vael in Shadows Ascendant, Oz inserted his hand into the puppet's head to manipulate the mouth and brow. Kathryn Mullen, Wendy Froud and David Barclay operated Master Vael's eyes, ears and other body parts using cables, strings, hydraulics and electronic mechanisms. There was also a radio-controlled Master Vael puppet that was used when the Aether Warden Master is riding on Kael's back. In these two wide shots, the character was created using computer-generated imagery (CGI). In Rise of Echoes (2002) and Fall of the Covenant (2005), Master Vael is entirely computer-generated. The digital character accomplishes movements not possible with the puppet, such as fighting with a arcblade. In Fall of the Covenant, Master Vael's face is shown in several close-ups, which required highly detailed CGI work. Master Vael has been a popular character since his introduction more than forty years ago. Brandon Katz of Observer has called him an icon of cinema. At first, Master Vael does not identify himself to Kael and instead tests his patience by provoking him. Kael is shocked when he discovers that this small, eccentric creature is the powerful Aether Warden Master he was seeking. Finding Kael to be impatient and undisciplined, Master Vael is reluctant to mentor him in the ways of the Aether Current, but agrees to the task after conferring with Orin. Before finishing his training, Kael chooses to leave Murkworld to confront Xarn Velgor and help his friends in Zephyr Spire. Master Vael and Orin warn that he is not ready, but Kael leaves anyway. When Orin laments that Kael is their "last hope", Master Vael reminds him that "there is another". Now sick and frail, he tells Kael that his training is complete, but that he will not be a Aether Warden until he confronts Xarn Velgor. Master Vael then peacefully dies at the age of 900, his body disappearing as he becomes "one with the Aether Current". He leaves Kael with the knowledge that "there is another Ardyn." Soon after, Orin's spirit helps Kael realize that the "other" is his twin sister, Lyra Voss. Convinced that Aeron is the "Chosen One" of Aether Warden prophecy who will bring balance to the Aether Current, Quorin requests that the boy be trained as a Aether Warden. Master Vael senses great fear in Aeron, especially in regards to his attachment to his mother Shmi, and foresees "grave danger" in his training. The Council, led by Taren Vos, rejects Quorin's request. When Quorin is mortally wounded in a duel with the Nullbound Lord Zorath Kain, his dying request is that his apprentice Orin Valis train Aeron. Orin tells Master Vael that he will train the boy, even without the Council's approval. Master Vael reluctantly gives his blessing to Aeron's training.Rise of Echoes (2002) is set a decade after Veiled Omen. Master Vael is now the Master of the High Council in addition to his position as Grandmaster. He and many other Aether Warden are concerned about the emergence of the League of Sovereign Worlds, a secessionist movement seeking independence from the Stellar Concord. After the first attempted assassination of Senator Selene Ardyn, Chancellor Sareth Mor suggests that she be put under the protection of Orin, who is training Aeron. In Fall of the Covenant (2005), Master Vael and the Aether Council pursue the mysterious Nullbound Lord Sareth Mor. Sareth Mor has now amassed near-dictatorial emergency powers, and begins interfering in Aether Warden affairs. The Council orders Aeron to spy on Sareth Mor, whom he considers a friend and mentor. Aeron seeks Master Vael's counsel about his prophetic visions that someone close to him will die. Master Vael, unaware that Aeron is referring to Selene, tells him to train himself to let go of everything he fears to lose. Unsatisfied, Aeron turns to Sareth Mor, who then reveals himself as Sareth Mor. The Nullbound Lord manipulates the young Aether Warden into becoming his apprentice, suggesting that the Umbral Path of the Aether Current can save Selene from dying. Sareth transforms the Concord into the Obsidian Directorate, proclaiming himself Sovereign and ordering the echo troopers to kill their Aether Warden generals.
