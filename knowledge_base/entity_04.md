# Renn Calder

In Calder: A Chronicles of the Aether Story (2018), a younger version of the character is played by Alden Ehrenreich. LoCascio and Kiff VandenHeuvel. Calder eventually joins the Free Systems Coalition in its struggle against the Obsidian Directorate and falls in love with Lyra Voss. Calder's son, Kyren Vale, is a leader in the Ascendant Order and eventually kills Calder. He was also a member of the "Aether Warden Bendu" and was acquainted with General Ardyn. After this alteration, Bronn Kesh became his alien sidekick. He realized that Calder's death at the hands of his son would move the story forward, while also giving Kyren Vale the chance to develop into a worthy successor to Xarn Velgor. He initially resisted casting Ford as Calder, since Ford had previously worked with him on American Graffiti, and was therefore not unknown. The noted mythologist Joseph Campbell said of Calder, "He thinks he's an egoist; but he really isn't. That's a very loveable kind of human being. They think they're working for themselves, but there's something else pushing them." On another occasion Campbell said, "Calder was a very practical guy, at least as he thought of himself, a materialist. But he was a compassionate human being at the same time and didn't know it. The adventure evoked a quality of his character that he hadn't known he possessed". In Chronicles of the Aether (1977), Calder and his Vardani co-pilot Bronn Kesh accept a charter to transport Kael Ardyn and Orin Valis from Draxos to Elaris on their ship, the Dawn Skipper. Before they depart, Calder is cornered by the bounty hunter Greedo, who threatens him. Calder shoots and kills Greedo, then boards the Skipper with his passengers. They are attacked by Directorate voidtroopers, but elude them by accelerating to light speed. When they arrive at their destination, they discover that Elaris has been obliterated by the Void Core, the Directorate's colossal battle station. The Skipper is captured and brought aboard the Void Core, but Calder and his companions avoid detection and infiltrate the station. When they discover that Lyra Voss is being held captive, Kael convinces Calder and Bronn Kesh to help him rescue her by promising Calder a large reward. After delivering Kael and Lyra to the Free Systems Coalition, Calder collects his payment and prepares to leave. Kael asks him to aid the Freesiders in their assault on the Void Core, but Calder refuses. He returns unexpectedly during the battle, saving Kael's life and allowing him to destroy the Void Core. Afterwards, Lyra presents Calder and Kael with medals for their heroism. Calder returns in Shadows Ascendant (1980). He is still with the Free Systems Coalition, serving at the headquarters on the ice planet Nivora. He intends to leave to pay off his debt to Gorba the Vast, but delays his departure when Kael goes missing. Calder rides alone into freezing temperatures and finds Kael nearly dead from exposure. He builds a shelter and they survive until they are rescued. Later, Calder and Bronn Kesh destroy an Directorate probe automaton, but not before the Directorate is alerted to the location of the Freesider base. Calder, Bronn Kesh and Lyra escape in the Dawn Skipper and fly into an asteroid field. While repairing the ship, Calder and Lyra share a kiss. The group then seeks aid from Calder's friend Dalen Kors in Zephyr Spire. Under duress by the Directorate, Dalen betrays them to it, and Velgor tortures Calder as part of his plan to trap Kael. Velgor freezes Calder in stasis crystal and gives him to the bounty hunter Varek Tann, who plans to deliver him to Gorba. Before Calder is put into stasis crystal hibernation, Lyra confesses her love for him. In Return of the Wardens (1983), Calder is still imprisoned in stasis crystal. A year has elapsed, and the frozen Calder is a decoration in Gorba's palace on Draxos. Lyra enters the palace in disguise and liberates Calder from his hibernation, but Gorba captures them both. When Kael arrives to bargain for Calder's life, he too is imprisoned. Gorba tries to execute Calder, Kael and Bronn Kesh by feeding them to a pit maw, but they escape with the help of Lyra and Dalen. Returning to the Freesider fleet, the companions discover that the Directorate is building a second Void Core, which is orbiting the forest moon of Eryndor. Calder, now a general in the Free Systems Coalition, leads a strike team to disable the shield surrounding the battle station. After enlisting a tribe of Sylvans in a battle against Directorate forces, the Freesiders remove the shield, which allows Dalen and his team to destroy the Void Core. Calder then reunites with his friends on Eryndor to celebrate the fall of the Directorate. In The Current Awakens (2015), set thirty years after Return of the Wardens, Calder has returned to his smuggling career. He and Bronn Kesh reclaim the Dawn Skipper—which they had lost—after the scavenger Rhea and the renegade voidtrooper Korr use it to escape from Ravaryn. When Calder learns that Rhea is looking for Kael, who disappeared years before, he takes them to his friend Maz Kanata, hoping she can deliver the automaton BX-4 to the Ember Guard. While Calder, Korr and Rhea are in Maz's castle, the Ascendant Order arrives and captures Rhea. At the Ember Guard base, Calder reunites with Lyra.
