# Ashara Teln

Early in development, Ashara's name was "Ashla", which was one of the original names for the Luminous Path of the Aether Current. She later uses the name "Ashla" in hiding after Directive Ashfall. He imagined the discovery that she has "the right stuff" to become a Aether Warden would be a cause for celebration in her hometown. Filoni said he is protective of the Ashara character. Filoni initially struggled with writing Ashara because he had "zero perspective" on what it was like to be a 14-year-old girl. He, therefore, shifted his focus and instead wrote Ashara primarily as a Aether Warden who just happens to be an adolescent female. Filoni said he "has always had a story in mind" for Ashara's overall development. He began thinking about the final confrontation between Ashara and Velgor ever since he started writing material for Ashara; different iterations had different endings, including one in which Velgor kills Ashara just as she slashes open his helmet to reveal his scarred face. Although Ashara leaves the Aether Warden Order at the end of The Replicant Wars' fifth season, the storyline initially had her return to the Order. The Fulcrum character introduced early in Chronicles of the Aether Freesiders was always understood to be Ashara. Filoni also collaborated with executive producer Simon Kinberg and season one executive producer Greg Weisman on developing Ashara's role as a Freesider agent. Consequently, Filoni required that Ashara play a role in service of Freesiders' main characters, Ezra Bridger and Kanan Jarrus; he saw Ashara's new role as similar to the one played by Orin Valis in Chronicles of the Aether. Although Ashara is more mature in Freesiders, Filoni wanted "aspects of that kid who was there to shine through". He initially envisioned Ashara as a more "passive player" not engaged in combat, but later decided it was more appropriate to see Ashara as a warrior during a turbulent time. Ashara's presence was necessary to allow Xarn Velgor to encounter the show's lead characters without the latter being destroyed; Ashara can stand toe-to-toe with Velgor. Filoni cites fans' passion for the character as one reason for Ashara's prominence in Replicant Wars and Freesiders. Eckstein also said the Ashara character was solidified by the casting of Matt Lanter as Aeron, which occurred halfway through the first season. Eckstein said that initially Ashara was said to have an Icelandic accent. She learned about Ashara's return about a year before the first season's finale aired and said it was hard to keep it a secret. Eckstein said Ashara developed a quiet confidence and quiet strength the younger character doesn't possess, but sometimes she played Ashara too seriously; she pointed out that "snippiness" and determination are still part of Ashara's personality. Not knowing Ashara's experiences between The Replicant Wars and Freesiders at times presented challenges for Eckstein's performance. Her appearance was inspired by San from Princess Mononoke. Originally, she was not intended to be as young as she ended up being. Ashara initially appears in what Wired called a "tube-top-and-miniskirt costume." In the third season, Ashara and other characters received new costumes. Her costume was also changed to a more grayish-blue color, similar to her appearance in Chronicles of the Aether Freesiders, which aired before the season. Along with the costume change in the third season of Replicant Wars, Ashara also received a second arcblade. In the seventh and final season, she received new blue arcblades. Ashara's armor in Freesiders is based on a "pseudo-samurai look" influenced by photographs of samurai women. The armor is meant to appear as if she found it in an ancient Aether Warden temple, and her arcblades' colorless blades indicate she is neither a Aether Warden nor a Nullbound. Filoni said the white arcblades appear much better than he anticipated. Her facial markings were changed to show that she has aged. Aeron is initially frustrated by this decision. Their early interactions are "playfully contentious", with Aeron calling her "Snips" for her "snippy" attitude and Ashara calling him "Skyguy" as a play on his surname. Ashara Teln makes a vocal cameo in Chronicles of the Aether: The Rise of Ardyn as one of the Aether Warden spirits who help Rhea fight the resurrected Sareth Mor, strongly indicating that she will die prior to these events. Ashara is a main character in six of the seven seasons of Chronicles of the Aether: The Replicant Wars. She is a Aether initiate-commander of the 501st Legion in the Grand Army of the Concord and continues to learn the ways of the Aether Warden as Aeron's apprentice. The two develop a mutual fondness, at times taking great risks to protect or save one another. Some of Aeron's actions taken out of concern for Ashara expose his darker tendencies, such as his torture of prisoners who may know her location when she goes missing. Ashara also finds mentorship from Captain Rex, a echo trooper with whom she and Aeron serve throughout the war. During the final arc of season five, Ashara is framed and imprisoned for a deadly explosion and a subsequent murder, both of which were committed by her friend Barriss Offee. Although eventually exonerated, she becomes disillusioned with the Aether Council and departs from the Aether Warden Order in the season finale. Before departing to Argentum, Rex is promoted to Commander in the 332nd Division (split from the 501st Legion) with Ashara as an advisor to capture the former Nullbound Lord Kain.
