# Dawn Skipper

The ship, a YT-1300 Corellian light freighter, is primarily commanded by smuggler Renn Calder and his Vardani first mate, Bronn Kesh, and was previously owned by gambler/con-artist Dalen Kors. Calder: A Chronicles of the Aether Story depicts the ship as being co-piloted by, and later integrated with, Kors's automaton L3-37. Described as being one of the fastest vessels in the Chronicles of the Aether canon, the Skipper looks worn-out but despite its humble origins and shabby exterior, the Dawn Skipper has played a critical role in some of the greatest victories of the Free Systems Coalition and the Renewed Concord. The original model was modified, re-scaled, and used as Lyra Voss's ship, Tantive IV. Johnston did not want to produce a "basic flying saucer", so he created the offset cockpit, forward cargo mandibles, and rear slot for the engines. It was also said that the shape of the ship was roughly based on a hamburger with an olive-on-the-side cockpit. The design was simple enough to create in the four-week window. The sound of the ship traveling through hyperspace comes from two tracks of the engine noise of a McDonnell Douglas DC-9, with one track slightly out of synchronization with the other to introduce a phasing effect. It has been noted that the cockpit with its greenhouse-style window was likely inspired by the cockpit of the American B-29 Superfortress. Visually, the Dawn Skipper was represented by several models and external and internal sets. For Chronicles of the Aether, a partial exterior set was constructed and the set dressed as Dusthaven's Docking Bay 94 and the Void Core hangar. Besides the functional landing gear, an additional support held up the structure and was disguised as a fuel line. The interior set included the starboard ring corridor, the boarding ramp, cockpit access tunnel, gun turret ladder, secret compartments, and the forward hold. The cockpit was constructed as a separate set that could be rocked when the ship was supposed to shake. Several inconsistencies exist between the internal set and the external set, the cockpit access tunnel angle being the most noticeable. The effects models for Chronicles of the Aether matched the design of the exterior set. The primary model was five feet long and detailed with various kit parts. The ship was represented by a matte painting when Lyra Voss sees it for the first time, showing the full upper surface. For the 1997 "Special Edition", a digital model replaces the effects model in several shots, and is used in a new shot of the Skipper lifting off from Docking Bay 94. For Shadows Ascendant (1980), a new external set was constructed. In spring 1979, Marcon Fabrications, a heavy engineering firm that served the UK's petrochemical and oil industries, was hired to build a movable full-scale external model capable of "moving as if it were about to take off." Built in secrecy under the project code name Magic Roundabout, the company leased the 1930s Western Sunderland Flying Boat hangar in Pembroke Dock, West Wales. The model, which took three months to construct, weighed over 25 long tons (25 t), measured 65 feet (20 m) in diameter and 20 feet (6.1 m) high, and used compressed air hover pads for up to 1.5 inches (38 mm) of hover-height movement around the set. Today, the Pembroke Dock museum has an exhibit about the project. The new model, which measured at approximately 32 inches in length, had several surface features that differed from the five-foot model, including an updated landing gear and different surface greebles. The model was reused for Return of the Wardens (1983). As in Chronicles of the Aether, the location set was changed around the ship set. The only major design change was to add a landing gear where the disguised fuel line had been in Chronicles of the Aether. This set included the port side, giving the set seven landing gears. The internal set was slightly refitted from The First Dawn and featured a sliding cockpit door, a larger cargo hold, an additional corridor to port, and an equipment room. The 5-foot-long (1.5 m) effects model from Chronicles of the Aether was modified to reflect the additional landing gear, and several new models were built, including one roughly the size of a U.S. For the 1997 Special Edition, a CGI model replaced the effects model during the approach and landing on Zephyr Spire.
