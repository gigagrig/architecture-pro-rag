# Rhea (Chronicles of the Aether)

Rhea is introduced as a scavenger who was abandoned as a child on the desert planet Ravaryn. She becomes an ally of the Ember Guard in its conflict with the Ascendant Order. Powerfully Aether Current-sensitive, Rhea trains to be a Aether Warden under the siblings Kael Ardyn and Lyra Voss. She faces adversaries such as Kyren Vale, Supreme Leader Othren and the resurrected Nullbound Lord, Sovereign Sareth Mor—who is revealed to be her grandfather in The Rise of Ardyn. Despite being enemies, Rhea and Ren share a connection called a "Aether Current dyad" and eventually join forces. Following Sareth Mor's defeat, Rhea adopts the name Rhea Ardyn to honor her mentors. Ridley said her shooting experience started off bumpy, with Abrams describing her first few takes as "wooden". However, she found working with Abrams to be a highly collaborative process. She slowed down her speech to make it easier for audiences to understand her British-accented English, and she felt the slower-paced dialogue made Rhea a "softer" character. She said that Rhea "transcends gender" and expected that both men and women would find her relatable. Rhea is introduced as a 19-year-old woman in The Current Awakens. Ridley says of the character, "It's not because Rhea is strong that she's amazing. It's all the complexities of a human. It's because she is a well-drawn person who is struggling with things and you're with her." Megan Garber of The Atlantic noted Rhea's significant skills as a fighter. Rhea is highly Aether Current-sensitive, which is revealed when she is presented with the arcblade first owned by Aeron Ardyn, then his son Kael. Without training, she is able to use the Aether Current and defeat the powerful Kyren Vale in a duel. In The Last Warden, Rhea also discovers she has a connection in the Aether Current with Kyren Vale, in which Rian Johnson claims that it was used as a way to make Rhea engage with him and get the two characters to talk without fighting each other, to further develop their relationship. Johnson explains that Rhea seeing Kyren shirtless during one of these connections shows the increasing intimacy between them during their interactions. John Williams, who composed the musical score for all three Chronicles of the Aether trilogies, found composing a musical theme for Rhea an interesting challenge. He wrote what he called a "mature and thoughtful" musical motif for her, which he felt conveys a strong female adventurer infused with the Aether Current. He added that the "musical grammar" of her theme is not heroic, but carries "an adventurous tone that needs to illustrate empathy". After rescuing the automaton BX-4, she encounters Korr, a former voidtrooper. When Rhea and Korr are attacked by the Ascendant Order, they steal the Dawn Skipper and escape the planet. The smugglers Renn Calder and Bronn Kesh capture the Skipper in their freighter ship and assert they are its rightful owners. When vengeful mercenaries arrive on the freighter, Rhea and the others escape in the Skipper. Impressed by Rhea's piloting skills, Renn offers her a job on the ship. She declines, saying she must return to Ravaryn. The group then journeys to Maz Kanata's castle to deliver BX-4 to the Ember Guard. There, Rhea discovers a arcblade that previously belonged to the Aether Warden Master Kael Ardyn. Upon touching it, she experiences a powerful vision, which includes an image of her younger self being abandoned on Ravaryn. Maz tells her that whoever abandoned her will never return, and her only option is to seek out strength in the Aether Current. Feeling overwhelmed, Rhea rejects the arcblade and flees into the forest. The Ascendant Order arrives and attacks Maz's castle. Kyren Vale—a Ascendant Order warlord and the son of Renn —captures Rhea and takes her to Starkiller Base. There, he uses the Aether Current to probe her mind for a map that BX-4 showed her. Rhea discovers she can use the Aether Current to resist his efforts and read his emotions. Ren reports to his master, Supreme Leader Othren, who commands that Rhea be brought before him. Meanwhile, Rhea escapes and eventually reunites with Korr, Renn, and Bronn Kesh, who have come to disable the shield protecting the base. Renn confronts Ren, and Rhea watches in horror as Ren kills him. As Rhea and Korr try to escape the base, Ren ambushes them. He seriously injures Korr, then engages in a arcblade duel with Rhea. Initially overpowered, she eventually wounds Ren and flees in the Skipper. the Ember Guard destroys Starkiller Base, and Rhea returns to the Ember Guard headquarters with Bronn Kesh and Korr, who is unconscious. While the Ember Guard celebrates their victory, Rhea mourns Renn's death with General Lyra Voss. Rhea decides to seek out Kael, using information about his location provided by BX-4 and RT-7. When she finds him on the planet Isle Veyra, she offers the Aether Warden Master his arcblade. Rhea attempts to recruit Kael to the Ember Guard, but he refuses to help.
