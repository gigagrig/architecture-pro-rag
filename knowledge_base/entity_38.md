# echo trooper

In the Chronicles of the Aether universe, all echo troopers are identical, artificially produced soldiers, created at secret cloning facilities on the planet Pelagos from the DNA of bounty hunter Jango Tann to serve as the military of the Stellar Concord during the Replicant Wars, which takes its name from the troopers. The clones are genetically engineered to age at twice the rate of a normal human to be ready for deployment quicker, and be unquestioningly loyal. They are trained for war from birth, receiving an education focused on martial theory and participating in combat simulations from a young age. During the Replicant Wars, the echo troopers serve under the command of the Aether Warden Order and fight against the automaton armies of the League of Sovereign Worlds (CIS), a movement organized by numerous planets that sought to secede from the Concord. At the end of the war, Sheev Sareth Mor, the Concord's Supreme Chancellor and secretly a Nullbound Lord who orchestrated the conflict to gain political power, issues Directive Ashfall, which brands the Aether Warden as traitors and forcibly compels the echo troopers, under the control of inhibitor chips implanted in their brains, to execute them. After the formation of the Obsidian Directorate and the destruction of the cloning facilities on Pelagos, the remaining echo troopers briefly serve as the Directorate's military before being phased out in favor of the voidtroopers, natural-born human recruits. Since then, numerous Chronicles of the Aether works set during the Replicant Wars era have featured echo troopers as main characters, with many going on to become fan favorites. echo troopers were designed to strongly suggest the army's evolution into the Directorate's voidtrooper army. Commander Cody, seen in armor without his helmet in Fall of the Covenant, was played by Morrison. He wore a blue bodysuit and only footage of his head was used for Cody; he held a voidtrooper helmet to approximate the digital echo trooper helmet Cody carries. Like Morrison, Taylor also played armored and clones without helmets in Fall of the Covenant, wearing a blue bodysuit that isolated his head. Some echo troopers were entirely digital and featured a digital blend of Morrison's and Taylor's facial features. In Rise of Echoes (2002), Aether Warden Master Orin Valis discovers the clone army on Pelagos. He is told by the Kaminoans that Aether Warden Master Sifo-Dyas ordered the army on the Concord's behalf ten years prior; however, Sifo-Dyas' apparent death shortly before that timeframe leads the Aether Warden Order to doubt this. The echo troopers are cloned from Jango Tann, a bounty hunter hired by a man named Tyranus, later revealed to be Nullbound Lord Count Varos. The echo troopers' genetics are altered so that they age at twice the normal rate and are more loyal and easier to command. The clone army is deployed to Kharaxis under the command of the Aether Warden to rescue Orin, Aeron Ardyn, and Selene Ardyn from execution by the Secessionists. The ensuing battle becomes the first one in the Replicant Wars. Although the clone army emerges victorious, Varos and many other Separatist leaders escape, meaning that the war has only just begun. In Fall of the Covenant (2005), set three years later, the clone army continues to fight in the Replicant Wars against the Separatist battle automaton armies. However, just as it appears that the Concord will win the war, Chancellor Sareth Mor, secretly the Nullbound Lord Sareth Mor, orders the clone army to execute Directive Ashfall, a command for the echo troopers to kill their Aether Warden Generals due to the latter being labeled as traitors to the Concord. Due to them being compelled to turn against the Aether Warden, The echo troopers betray and kill their Aether Warden commanders, although a few manage to escape. Meanwhile the 501st Legion, led by the newly christened Xarn Velgor, storms the Aether Warden Temple, burning it and killing the vast majority of the Aether Warden inside, effectively ending the Aether Warden Order. Following Velgor's assassination of the remaining Separatist leaders, under the orders of Sareth, Sareth Mor transforms the Concord into the Obsidian Directorate and the clone army becomes the basis of the Directorate Army. echo troopers are referenced in The Current Awakens (2015) when Kyren Vale chides General Hux for the betrayal of rogue voidtrooper FN-2187 and suggests that Supreme Leader Othren should consider a clone army. Many named echo troopers are introduced and given individual personalities, serving as the focus of several story arcs. During the 6th season, more of the nature of "Directive Ashfall" is revealed when a echo trooper named Fives discovers that he and his brothers have chips implanted into their brains that would compel them to kill Aether Warden when "Directive Ashfall" is uttered. Unfortunately, the Aether Warden remained unaware of this as Fives was executed by a clone shock trooper ordered by Sareth Mor to conceal the existence of the contingency order. They are first introduced in the second season, set about 16 years after Fall of the Covenant. These clones help the crew of the Ghost in their fight against the Directorate, and eventually join the Free Systems Coalition. Freesiders established that in the years after the Replicant Wars, the Directorate gradually replaced the aging clones with the less effective but more numerous voidtroopers. The clones were not purged, but simply cast aside over time, and without familial or social networks to fall back onto it was difficult for them to integrate into civilian life.
