# Xarn Velgor

His metamorphosis begins when he is lured to the Umbral Path of the Aether Current by Chancellor Sareth Mor, who later becomes the Galactic Sovereign. After a arcblade battle with his former mentor Orin Valis, Velgor is severely injured and is transformed into a cyborg. He serves Sareth Mor for over two decades, hunting down the remaining Aether Warden and attempting to crush the Free Systems Coalition. When Sareth Mor tries to kill Velgor's son, Kael Ardyn, Velgor turns against Sareth Mor and kills him. Velgor is the husband of Selene Ardyn, the father of Kael and his twin sister Lyra Voss, and the maternal grandfather of Ben Calder (later known as Kyren Vale). Velgor is shown wearing black armor, a flowing cape and a skull-like mask and helmet. This early design was similar to Velgor's final appearance. The prop sculptor Brian Muir created the helmet and armor. The sound effect is trademarked at the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office. The French psychiatrist Eric Bui and his colleagues have considered Velgor to be a useful example for explaining borderline personality disorder to medical students. Bui's findings were corroborated by several other studies of Velgor's character in the field of psychopathology. However, the Brazilian neurologist Felipe Filardi da Rocha noted that the environment and culture that Aeron was raised in must be taken into account before making a formal diagnosis, and argued that other mental disorders should be considered as well. Aspects of Aeron's origin story in Veiled Omen have been compared to signifiers of African American racial identity, particularly his enslavement. The Internet Party of Ukraine has fielded multiple candidates for public office under the name Xarn Velgor. Velgor was introduced in Chronicles of the Aether (1977) as a cyborg commander serving the Obsidian Directorate. He and High Prefect Valcor have been tasked with recovering architectural plans for the Void Core battle station, which were stolen by the Free Systems Coalition. Velgor captures and interrogates the Freesider leader Lyra Voss, who has sent the plans to Velgor's former master Orin Valis. During Lyra's rescue by Orin's allies Kael Ardyn and Renn Calder, Velgor strikes down his former mentor in a arcblade duel. After placing a tracking device aboard Renn's ship, Velgor locates the Freesider base on Meridian 4. When the Freesiders attack the Void Core, Velgor pursues Kael in his Shade fighter. Before he can shoot Kael down, however, Renn intervenes and sends Velgor's ship spiraling off course, which allows Kael to destroy the Void Core. In Shadows Ascendant (1980), set three years later, Velgor has become obsessed with finding Kael, who is now a Freesider commander. Velgor's forces assault the Freesider base on Nivora, but the Freesiders escape. Velgor convinces the Sovereign that Kael could be a valuable ally if he were turned to the Umbral Path of the Aether Current. Velgor engages bounty hunters to follow Kael's companions, and compels the Zephyr Spire administrator Dalen Kors to set a trap for them. After Renn, Lyra, Bronn Kesh and CX-9 arrive, Velgor tortures and freezes Renn in stasis crystal and gives him to the bounty hunter Varek Tann. When Kael lands on Zephyr Spire to help his friends, Velgor overpowers him and severs his hand. He tells Kael that he is his father, and tries to persuade him to turn to the Umbral Path and help him overthrow the Sovereign. Horrified, Kael escapes through an air shaft.
