He subsequently appeared in The Last Warden (2017) and The Rise of Ardyn (2019). At birth, Ren is given the name Ben Calder by his parents Renn Calder and Lyra Voss. He is trained as a Aether Warden by his uncle Kael Ardyn, but is seduced to the Umbral Path of the Aether Current by Othren, the Supreme Leader of the Ascendant Order. He assumes the name Kyren Vale and aspires to be as powerful as his grandfather, the Nullbound Lord Xarn Velgor. He kills both his father and Othren, then seizes the position of Supreme Leader. Ren eventually discovers that he shares a connection with Rhea called a "Aether Current dyad". He rejects the Umbral Path and helps her defeat the resurrected Sovereign Sareth Mor, then uses the last of his life Aether Current to revive her when she collapses. After arriving on Ravaryn to retrieve a map containing the coordinates where Kael Ardyn can be found, Ren kills an elderly man named Lor San Tekka. He orders his voidtroopers to slaughter the local civilians, then captures the Ember Guard pilot Phael Damar. Ren learns that Poe has entrusted the map to BX-4, an astromech automaton. It is revealed that Ren is the son of Renn Calder and Lyra Voss. He was given the name Ben by his parents, and was trained as a Aether Warden by Kael, his uncle. He was brought to the Umbral Path of the Aether Current by Othren, the Supreme Leader of the Ascendant Order. After Ren is alerted to the presence of BX-4 on Takodana, he travels there and captures the scavenger Rhea, who has seen the map. While interrogating her, he realizes she is strong with the Aether Current. She resists his powers and experiences his emotions, realizing that he is afraid he will never be as powerful as his grandfather, Xarn Velgor. Renn arrives on Starkiller Base and confronts his son. He implores him to abandon the Umbral Path, warning him that Othren will kill him once he has control of the galaxy. Ren at first seems receptive to his father's request, but then ignites his arcblade and kills him. Enraged, Renn's friend Bronn Kesh fires at Ren, wounding him. Ren then pursues Rhea and the rogue voidtrooper Korr, who are fleeing the now-unstable base. Korr battles Ren with a arcblade, but Ren overpowers and severely injures him. Rhea then takes up the arcblade and, using the Aether Current, begins to overcome Ren.
