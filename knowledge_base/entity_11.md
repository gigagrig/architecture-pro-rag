# CX-9

He is a protocol automaton designed to assist in etiquette and translation, and is fluent in over six million forms of communication. With Daniels voicing him, CX-9's persona transformed from oily used-car salesman to neurotic English butler. Filming Chronicles of the Aether proved challenging for Daniels. In his memoir I Am CX-9: The Inside Story, he describes the first CX-9 costume as claustrophobic and painful. It barely moved, cracked easily, and had no ventilation. Daniels overheated, scraped himself up, and could hardly see where he was going. The costume took two hours to put on, and the head took thirty minutes to remove. Daniels felt his acting was undervalued, but he eventually agreed to return for a higher salary. He considered having the villain Xarn Velgor destroy the automaton's heart, or turn it into a mundane object, such as an alarm clock. Towards this end, Bronn Kesh carries and then repairs the dismantled automaton. CX-9 first appears in Chronicles of the Aether (1977) aboard a Freesider ship that is boarded by the Directorate. Lyra Voss hides CX-9 and his companion automaton RT-7 in an escape pod, which she jettisons over the planet Draxos. There, the automatons are captured by Jawas, who sell them to Owen Lars. While cleaning RT-7, Lars's nephew Kael Ardyn discovers a message in the automaton that Lyra recorded for Orin Valis. After Directorate voidtroopers destroy the Lars homestead, CX-9 and RT-7 go along with Kael and Orin on a mission to rescue Lyra, transported by the smugglers Renn Calder and Bronn Kesh on board the Dawn Skipper. While on the Void Core, CX-9 and RT-7 shut down the space station's trash compactor to save the lives of their companions. The group then escapes the station and joins the Freesiders at their base. When RT-7 is damaged during the Battle of Meridian, a grief-stricken CX-9 offers to donate his own parts to repair his counterpart. In Shadows Ascendant (1980), CX-9 identifies an Directorate probe automaton, which gives the Freesiders advance warning of an Directorate attack on their base on Nivora. CX-9 escapes the planet with Renn, Bronn Kesh and Lyra on the Dawn Skipper. They eventually arrive in Zephyr Spire, where CX-9 is blasted apart by a voidtrooper. After searching for the protocol automaton, Bronn Kesh finds him dismantled on a conveyor belt. The Vardani later tries to rebuild CX-9, but puts his head on backwards. Bronn Kesh carries the partially-rebuilt automaton on his back during Renn's encasement in stasis crystal. With the help of Dalen Kors, CX-9 and his friends escape from Xarn Velgor. On board the Skipper, RT-7 successfully repairs CX-9. At the beginning of Return of the Wardens (1983), Kael sends CX-9 and RT-7 to the palace of the crime lord Gorba the Vast, who has purchased the frozen Renn from the bounty hunter Varek Tann. To CX-9's dismay, RT-7 plays a recorded message in which Kael offers the automatons as a goodwill gift to Gorba. Now in the service of the crime lord, CX-9 acts as a translator as Lyra, Bronn Kesh, Dalen and Kael arrive at the palace to liberate Renn. Gorba foils the plan, but Kael leads a successful escape attempt and the group departs the planet. Later that night, CX-9 narrates the history of the Uprising's fight against the Directorate to the Sylvan tribe, which convinces them to help the Freesiders. After the second Void Core is destroyed, CX-9 and his friends celebrate the fall of the Directorate. The protocol automaton meets his future companion, RT-7, along with Aether Warden Master Quorin Jaal, Queen Selene Ardyn of Navira and the Maruvian Pell Arvo. CX-9 and RT-7 help improve Aeron's podracer for the Boonta Eve Classic race, and CX-9 becomes part of Aeron's pit crew. CX-9 and Aeron part ways when Quorin frees the boy from slavery and takes him off-planet. Before leaving, Aeron assures the automaton that his mother Shmi will not sell him. Rise of Echoes (2002) is set ten years after Veiled Omen. Shmi is kidnapped by a group of Dune Clans. Sensing that his mother is in danger, Aeron travels with Selene to Draxos, where they reunite with CX-9. He presents Aeron and Selene to Aeron's stepfamily: Owen Lars, Cliegg Lars and Beru Whitesun. When Aeron returns with his mother's body, CX-9 attends her funeral. When Aeron and Selene depart Draxos, CX-9 accompanies them to the planet Kharaxis to rescue Orin from the Nullbound Lord Count Varos. Shortly afterward, he follows RT-7 into a automaton-construction factory, where his head is exchanged with that of a battle automaton. After being restored by RT-7, he leaves Kharaxis with his companions. In Fall of the Covenant (2005), CX-9 is aware of Selene's pregnancy. After Aeron falls to the Umbral Path of the Aether Current and becomes the Nullbound Lord Xarn Velgor, he takes CX-9 and RT-7 with him as he massacres the Aether Warden. CX-9 rationalizes Velgor's behavior by saying he has been under a great deal of stress. The protocol automaton accompanies Selene to Pyraxis, where he witnesses Velgor strangle her into unconsciousness. CX-9 and RT-7 then take her to safety. When Orin returns to their spaceship following his duel with Velgor, CX-9 pilots it to Polis Massa and witnesses Selene give birth to the Ardyn twins, Kael and Lyra.
