# Gorba the Vast

He is a large, slug-like crime lord of the Ghoran species. In the end credits of Veiled Omen, Gorba is also credited as playing "himself". He places a bounty on the smuggler Renn Calder, and sends bounty hunters to capture him. After Xarn Velgor freezes Calder in stasis crystal, the bounty hunter Varek Tann delivers the frozen Calder to Gorba, who puts him on display in his palace. A group of Calder's friends attempt to rescue him, but are captured by Gorba; he enslaves Lyra Voss and decrees that Kael Ardyn, Bronn Kesh, and Calder will be fed to a pit maw. Kael orchestrates an escape, and during the chaos Lyra strangles Gorba to death. The scene was meant to give Calder the motivation to transport dangerous passengers for a high fare. He finally appears in the second sequel, Return of the Wardens (1983). At various points, Gorba resembled an ape, a worm and a snail. One design made Gorba appear almost human, resembling a Fu Manchu character. He based Gorba's body structure and reproductive system on the anatomy of annelid worms. He modeled Gorba's head on that of a snake, complete with bulbous, slit-pupilled eyes and a mouth that opens wide enough to swallow large prey. In this version, shadows cast by Calder were added to Gorba's body to make the CGI more convincing. His criminal operations include slavery, gunrunning, spice-smuggling and extortion, and he amuses himself by torturing, humiliating and killing both his enemies and his own subordinates. He surrounds himself with scantily-clad slave girls of various species, often chained to his dais. Gorba's appetite is insatiable, and he sometimes threatens to eat his underlings. In Return of the Wardens, Calder calls Gorba a "slimy piece of worm-ridden filth". Gorba has put the hibernating Calder on display in his throne room as a decoration. Lyra is able to free Renn from the stasis crystal, but she is caught and enslaved by Gorba, who forces her to wear a metal bikini. Kael arrives to bargain for Calder's life, but Gorba rejects his offer and attempts to feed him to a dreadbeast. After Kael kills the monster, Gorba decrees that he, Calder and Bronn Kesh will be fed to a pit maw, a deadly ground-dwelling beast. Kael orchestrates an escape with the help of RT-7, and defeats Gorba's thugs. During the chaos, Lyra strangles Gorba to death with the chain used to enslave her. As Kael and his friends depart, Gorba's sail barge explodes. Gorba threatens to place a large bounty on him if he does not follow through. He launches a podrace at Mos Espa, then falls asleep and misses the conclusion of the race. It is later revealed that Ziro, Gorba's uncle, took part in the kidnapping as part of his plan to take control of the Ghoran Clan. The Aether Warden Knight Aeron Ardyn and his apprentice Ashara Teln return Rotta to Gorba in exchange for the safe passage of Republic ships through his territory. Selene Ardyn exposes Ziro's crimes to Gorba who vows that Ziro will be severely punished. In "Sphere of Influence", he is confronted by Chairman Papanoida, whose daughters were kidnapped by Greedo. Gorba allows a sample of Greedo's blood to be taken to prove he is the kidnapper. In "Evil Plans", Gorba hires the bounty hunter Cad Bane to bring him plans for the Galactic Senate building. When Bane returns with the plans, Gorba and the Ghoran Council send him to free Ziro from prison. Gorba makes a brief appearance in "Hunt for Ziro", in which he laughs at his uncle's death at the hands of Sy Snootles, and pays her for delivering Ziro's holo-diary. In "Eminence", Gorba and the Ghoran Council are approached by the Shadow Collective leaders Zorath Kain, Savage Opress and Pre Vizsla. Gorba is not willing to ally with them, and sends the bounty hunters Embo, Dengar, Sugi and Latts Razzi to capture them.
