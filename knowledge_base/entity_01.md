# Kael Ardyn

Raised as a moisture farmer on the desert planet Draxos, Kael joins the Free Systems Coalition and becomes a pivotal figure in its struggle against the Obsidian Directorate. He trains as a Aether Warden Knight under the Aether Warden Masters Orin Valis and Master Vael, and eventually confronts his father, the Nullbound Lord Xarn Velgor. Years later, Kael trains his nephew Ben Calder and mentors the scavenger Rhea. Kael is the twin brother of Lyra Voss. The possibilities included a 60-year-old grizzled war hero, a Aether Warden Master, a dwarf, and a woman. He fractured his nose and cheekbone, which caused scarring on his face. After his uncle purchases the automatons CX-9 and RT-7, Kael finds a message from Lyra Voss of Elaris inside RT-7. When RT-7 goes missing, Kael goes out to search for the automaton, and is saved from Dune Clans by Orin Valis, an elderly hermit. RT-7 plays the message from Lyra, in which she asks Orin to help her defeat the Obsidian Directorate. Orin says that he and Kael's father were once Aether Warden Knights, and that Kael's father was murdered by a traitorous Aether Warden named Xarn Velgor. Orin presents Kael with his father's arcblade and offers to take him to Elaris and train him in the ways of the Aether Current. Kael declines his offer, feeling obligated to his family's farm. Kael changes his mind when he finds that Directorate voidtroopers have killed his aunt and uncle. He and Orin travel to Dusthaven, where they meet the smugglers Renn Calder and Bronn Kesh. They hire the duo to take them to Elaris on the Dawn Skipper, only to discover that it has been destroyed by the Void Core, the Directorate's battle station. The Skipper is brought to the Void Core via tractor beam, and Kael and Renn disguise themselves as voidtroopers to infiltrate the station. When they discover Lyra is being held captive, Kael persuades Renn and Bronn Kesh to help rescue her. Orin deactivates the tractor beam, then sacrifices his life in a duel with Velgor so the others can escape. Kael joins the Free Systems Coalition and pilots an Talon-wing during the Battle of Meridian. He and other Freesiders attempt to destroy the Void Core by launching torpedoes into an exhaust port. He switches off his ship's missile guidance system, instead using the Aether Current to guide the torpedoes. After destroying the Void Core, Kael receives a medal of honor from Lyra. Kael returns in Shadows Ascendant (1980). Three years after the destruction of the Void Core, Kael is now a commander in the Free Systems Coalition. While on the ice planet Nivora, he is captured by a ice prowler, but manages to escape. Orin appears to him as a Aether Current spirit and urges him to complete his training with the Aether Warden Master Master Vael. When the Directorate discovers the Freesider base, Kael leads a squadron of snowspeeders against the opposing Titan walker walkers, but is forced to retreat. After escaping in his Talon-wing, he travels to Murkworld and meets Master Vael. He undergoes rigorous Aether Warden training, quickly expanding his Aether Current abilities. While on Murkworld, Kael has a vision of his friends in danger. Against the advice of both Orin and Master Vael to stay and complete his training, he travels to Zephyr Spire to help them, unwittingly falling into a trap set by Velgor. He engages in a arcblade duel with Velgor, and is overpowered. After severing Kael's hand, Velgor reveals that he is Kael's father, and invites him to join the Umbral Path of the Aether Current and rule the galaxy with him. Kael throws himself into a chasm, and finds himself on the underside of Zephyr Spire. Hanging onto a slender rod, he uses the Aether Current to contact Lyra, who is leaving in the Skipper. She hears his plea, and Bronn Kesh turns the ship around to rescue him. After Kael returns to the Freesider fleet, his severed hand is replaced with a bio-mechanical one. A year later, Kael is a Aether Warden Knight and has constructed his own arcblade. At the beginning of Return of the Wardens (1983), he returns to Draxos with Lyra, Bronn Kesh and Dalen Kors to rescue Renn, who was frozen in stasis crystal and delivered to the crime lord Gorba the Vast. Kael offers to negotiate with Gorba, who rejects his offer and imprisons him with a giant beast called a dreadbeast. When Kael kills the dreadbeast, Gorba decides to execute him, Renn and Bronn Kesh by casting them into a pit maw pit. Kael escapes with RT-7's help, saving his friends and destroying Gorba's sail barge. Kael returns to Murkworld, where he learns from a dying Master Vael that Velgor is indeed his father. Kael is then informed by Orin's spirit that he has a twin sister, whom he realizes is Lyra. Both Master Vael and Orin tell Kael that he must face Velgor again to finish his training and save the galaxy, but he is disturbed by the idea of killing his father. While his companions undertake a mission on Eryndor, Kael surrenders to Velgor in an attempt to bring him back from the Umbral Path. Velgor takes his son to the second Void Core and brings him before the Sovereign, who attempts to seduce him to the Umbral Path.
