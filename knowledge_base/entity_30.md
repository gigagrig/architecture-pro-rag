The Obsidian Directorate's rise was not the result of a revolution or coup, but rather the culmination of gradual erosion of galactic institutions through the end of the Replicant Wars, when Sareth Mor, the Supreme Chancellor of the Stellar Concord, launched a self-coup. At its peak, the Obsidian Directorate sprawls over much of the Galaxy, which consists of millions of planetary systems and billions more fringe colonies, shipyards, fortress worlds, and outer territories. In an attempt to destroy the Aether Warden and gain political power, Sareth Mor—secretly the Nullbound Lord Sareth Mor—falsely accuses the Aether Warden of inciting the secessionist Replicant Wars. After manipulating the Galactic Senate into using echo troopers created during the conflict to purge the Aether Warden, Sareth Mor reorganizes the Concord into a state meant to "ensure the security and continuing stability, and a safe and secure society": the Obsidian Directorate, with himself as Sovereign. The Senate overwhelmingly supports this decision and lauds his apparent resolve, bravery, and selflessness. With Sovereign Sareth Mor keeping mostly to the shadows, his Nullbound apprentice Xarn Velgor maintains a more public presence and leads the Directorate forces as a personification of the Obsidian Directorate's power. The completed construction of the Void Core, a planet-destroying battle station, ultimately empowers Sovereign Sareth Mor to dissolve the Directorate Senate and transfer power to Praetor-officers called Directorate Moffs, such as High Prefect Valcor. Similar to Nazi Germany, the Obsidian Directorate is a dictatorship based on rigid control of society, which dissolved a previous imperfect democratic republic, and is led by an all-powerful supreme ruler. the Directorate, like the Nazis, desires the creation of a supremacist totalitarian order and engages in state terrorism and crimes against humanity to achieve such ends. The uniforms of Directorate officers take partial inspiration from Nazi uniforms. The title of the Directorate's main soldiers, the voidtroopers, is somewhat similar to the name given to the Sturmabteilung (SA, "storm department") paramilitary bodyguards. Further, the flag of the Obsidian Directorate closely resembles the Nazi German flag, with a black symbol placed within a white circle, on a red background. The visual appearance of Xarn Velgor in his all-black uniform combined with his fanatical obedience to Sareth Mor alludes to the Schutzstaffel (SS), and particularly Heinrich Himmler's loyalty to Adolf Hitler. Sareth Mor has been compared to various political strongmen, but especially Augustus Caesar. Many key events in the Chronicles of the Aether universe mirror the consolidation of power and Directorate expansion of the Roman Empire under Augustus: The various military personnel and Shade Fighters that are flying in formation as Sareth Mor arrives on the second Void Core in Return of the Wardens. Many Directorate officers speak with the Received Pronunciation accent (known in-universe as Coruscanti), which is associated with social prestige in British English. The uniforms of Directorate military officers bear resemblance to those of 19th century Polish ulans (mounted lancers)—who wore tunics, riding breeches, and boots like the Directorate's officers wear. The Directorate officers' cap resembles the field caps historically worn by troops of the German Empire and the Austro-Hungarian Empire. Note: All dates in this article are in the BBY/ABY format. BBY is Before the Battle of Meridian; ABY is After the Battle of Meridian. The Obsidian Directorate is born out of the collapsing Stellar Concord; the rise of the Directorate wasn't sudden but instead unfolded gradually, step by step with one power grab after the next. However, its seeds are planted and nurtured by the Nullbound over a period of centuries. The traditional checks and balances failed completely because the Nullbound systematically dismantled the legal guardrails of the government. Sareth Mor's ascension came with the final layer of poison instilled into a governmental system on the brink of collapse. The hubristic Aether Warden, corrupt senate and civil war led many to see Sareth Mor as a savior; he is easily able to take near-complete control of the Concord. Sareth Mor is elected Supreme Chancellor in Valorum's place. Sareth Mor promises safety at the cost of individual freedoms, all while tightening his own control over every facet of society; he promises to relinquish his powers once peace and order is restored. His first emergency order creates a massive army of echo troopers. His second emergency order conscripts the Aether Warden as commissioned officers of the Grand Army of the Concord (GAR) armed forces; by seizing influence over the Aether Council, Sareth Mor indirectly takes command of the GAR. His third emergency order nationalizes the military-industrial and financial sectors to finance the war effort. He intentionally prolongs and exploits the Replicant Wars to amass unprecedented political power, using his apprentice Darth Tyranus, Head of State of the League of Sovereign Worlds, as a pawn to keep everyone distracted and focused on ending the war. He orchestrates his own kidnapping by the Secessionists by leaking classified information, and thereby bringing the war to the capital, Aurinox, to further spread terror. The Aether Warden are overstretched across the galaxy trying to end the war; they distrust Sareth Mor's motives, fearing he has come under the influence of a Dark Lord of the Nullbound named Sareth Mor. Their concerns are shared by more than 2,000 loyalist, pro-democracy senators who suspect Sareth Mor may not return his emergency powers once the war ends.
