# Phael Damar

Poe is an Talon-wing fighter pilot for the Ember Guard who inadvertently brings the renegade voidtrooper Korr and the Ravaryn scavenger Rhea into battle against the Ascendant Order. During development of The Current Awakens, the character that would become Phael Damar was referred to as "John Doe" and was envisioned as a black man in his 30s or 40s. Phael Damar was named after Abrams's assistant Morgan Damar. Some of Poe's comedic lines in The Current Awakens were improvised by Isaac. In The Current Awakens, Poe is a skilled Talon-wing fighter pilot for the Ember Guard. The son of Free Systems Coalition fighters, he is a commander in the Ember Guard's Starfighter Corps and "one of Lyra Voss's most-trusted operatives" who is headstrong and "can fly anything". He's been sent on a mission by a certain princess, and he ends up coming up across [Korr], and their fates are forever intertwined." Natalie Zutter and Chris Lough of Tor.com wrote, "Poe is that rare creature who knows exactly what he wants to do with his life." Isaac said of the character in 2015, "Poe's the kind of guy you want in the trenches with you. He's straightforward, he's honest, he's incredibly loyal and he's got some swagger to him." Katy Waldman wrote for Slate, "This is Phael Damar, star pilot, heartthrob, wiseass, ace leather jacket–bestower, Korr's OTP. Phael Damar is one charming fellow. He's a hero, but in a world where there is absolute good and absolute evil, Poe is a bit gray." Addressing Poe's early life as explored in Free Fall, Segura said: Poe feels a lot of responsibility—to his parents' legacy, mainly—and that doesn't always mix well with his own youthful curiosity and desire for adventure. It's the core of Poe's conflict, as he leaves home and sets out on this big adventure—he has to basically see everything through the prism of what his parents would want him to do, and I feel like that's a really universal idea—that we're often trying to square the things with the idea of what's expected of us, and we eventually learn that we have to create our own set of rules while still honoring what came before. But also I think she sees in him the potential for a truly great leader of the Ember Guard and beyond ... Poe's arc is one of evolving from a heroic soldier to a seasoned leader, to see beyond the single-mindedness of winning the battle to the larger picture of the future of the galaxy. [Lyra], with tough love, wants to push Poe to be more than the badass pilot, to temper his heroic impulses with wisdom and clarity. He is an Talon-wing fighter pilot and a member of the Ember Guard. He is sent by General Lyra Voss to the planet Ravaryn to retrieve part of a map that shows the location of her brother, Kael Ardyn. When the Ascendant Order arrives on Ravaryn, Poe hides the map in the automaton BX-4 and sends him into the desert. Poe is captured and tortured by Kyren Vale, who is searching for the map. A renegade voidtrooper liberates Poe, and they flee the Ascendant Order in a Shade fighter. During their flight, Poe gives the voidtrooper the name Korr. Their ship is hit with missiles, and crashes on Ravaryn. Korr is ejected from the ship and presumes that Poe is dead when the destroyed craft sinks into the sand. Poe later reappears leading an Talon-wing squadron in an assault on the Ascendant Order. After encountering the Blarina scavenger Naka, he helped him escape from a group of pirates. Naka then helped Poe get to Blowback Town. The Last Warden (2017) begins shortly after the destruction of Starkiller Base. As the Ascendant Order closes in on the Ember Guard headquarters, Poe leads a counterattack to give the Ember Guard time to evacuate. Once the transports are away, Lyra orders Poe to pull back, but he insists that they seize the opportunity to destroy the Ascendant Order Dreadnought. His team of bombers succeed in taking out the ship, but all the crews are killed. Lyra demotes Poe to Captain for recklessness and disobeying orders. Using a tracking device, the Ascendant Order pursues the Ember Guard through hyperspace and mounts another attack, which claims the lives of most of the Ember Guard leadership and renders Lyra unconscious. Poe assumes he will be named acting general, but Lyra instead promotes Vice Admiral Holdo, who dismisses Poe's suggestions due to his earlier reckless actions. Poe concocts a plan with Korr and the mechanic Rose Tico to disable the tracking device, but keeps the operation a secret from Holdo. When he discovers that Holdo plans to abandon a vulnerable ship, Poe stages a mutiny. Korr and Rose fail to disable the tracker, and an awakened Lyra stops Poe's uprising and resumes command. Despite his disobedience, both Lyra and Holdo express admiration for Poe's daring. the Ember Guard forces then arrive on the planet Salaris, where Poe leads an unsuccessful offensive against the Ascendant Order. While Kael distracts Ren, Poe leads the remaining members of the Ember Guard in an escape through an uncharted passage. In The Last Warden, Poe was originally going to accompany Korr on his mission to Canto Bight. He created the character Rose to challenge and contrast with Korr, and made her his companion instead.
