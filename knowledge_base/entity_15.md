# Dalen Kors

In Return of the Wardens (1983), Dalen helps rescue Renn from Gorba the Vast, and later becomes a general in the Free Systems Coalition. The character would initially support the Directorate—believing he could outsmart the Imperials—before coming to see the Uprising as a more worthy cause. The book The Art of Chronicles of the Aether: The Rise of Ardyn says the Aurebesh text on the cane reads "Baron Dalen Kors". Jonathan Kasdan, a co-writer of Calder: A Chronicles of the Aether Story, stated that Dalen is pansexual. Dalen Kors first appears in Shadows Ascendant as an old friend of Renn Calder. He is the previous owner of Renn's ship, the Dawn Skipper, and he is the administrator of Zephyr Spire. When Renn, Lyra Voss, Bronn Kesh, and CX-9 arrive at Zephyr Spire, Dalen welcomes them as guests, but soon betrays them to Xarn Velgor, who plans to use the group as bait to ensnare Kael Ardyn. Dalen reveals later that he had reluctantly agreed to betray them after Velgor threatened to put Zephyr Spire under Directorate control if he refused. Dalen allows Velgor to freeze Renn in stasis crystal, but when Velgor takes Lyra and Bronn Kesh prisoner, his conscience gets the better of him. In the ensuing evacuation of Zephyr Spire, Dalen helps the group escape in the Skipper. Soon after, he assists in rescuing Kael from the underside of Zephyr Spire. Afterwards, he promises to help find Renn. In Return of the Wardens, Dalen helps Kael rescue Renn from the crime lord Gorba the Vast. Later, Dalen is made a general in the Free Systems Coalition, and pilots the Dawn Skipper in an attack on the second Void Core. Needing a ship to steal a load of starship fuel, Renn tries to win the Skipper from Dalen in a game of sabacc, but Dalen cheats and beats Renn. However, Dalen agrees to join Renn's mission in exchange for a percentage of the profits. During the heist and subsequent escape, Dalen is injured. He abandons Renn's group, but Renn tracks him down and wins the Skipper from him. In "Idiot's Array", Dalen wins Chopper, the repair automaton of the crew of the Ghost, in a game of sabacc, forcing the crew to assist him with a dangerous smuggling run to get their automaton back. The crew become Dalen's reluctant business partners following the ordeal, leading to their first encounter with the crime boss Azmorigan. In "The Siege of Lothal", the crew of the Ghost approach Dalen for help in getting off of Lothal, which is under Directorate occupation. In Chronicles of the Aether: Outlaws (2024), which is set between Shadows Ascendant and Return of the Wardens, Dalen is depicted as a charismatic, smooth-talking gambler and entrepreneur. He interacts with the game's protagonist, Kay Vess, providing assistance and engaging in several missions. The Legends works comprise a separate narrative universe. Dalen uses Drebble's name as a cover identity, so that any animosity towards Dalen will be brought against Drebble. Anderson, frequently depict Dalen getting involved in a variety of entrepreneurial schemes. With his in-laws' money and his entrepreneurial abilities, he opens a mining facility on the Outer Rim planet of Dubrillion. In The New Aether Warden Order and beyond, Dalen continues to be a valuable ally and friend to the Ardyn-Calder family. The story begins shortly after Dalen wins the Dawn Skipper, along with a automaton, in a game of sabacc. Arriving on the planet Rafa IV to pick up the automaton, Dalen witnesses convicts harvesting mind-draining crystals which prolong the life of elite citizens. Dalen is arrested and brought before the sorcerer Rokur Gepta, who offers to spare his life if he can locate the legendary Mindharp of the lost Sharu civilization. After selling a load of life-crystals, and accompanied by his automaton Vuffi Raa, Dalen attempts a career as an honest freighter captain. After some bad luck, he becomes nostalgic for his old smuggling trade. He is invited to a sabacc game on the planet Oseon, and is followed by Rokur Gepta. During the game, Dalen is assaulted. He is put on trial for carrying a weapon, which is illegal on Oseon. He is offered a smuggling deal as an alternative to execution. Nearly a year after leaving the Oseon system, Dalen and Raa encounter the vacuum-breathing creature Lehesu, who hails from the ThonBoka nebula. A month later, Dalen and Raa receive word that ThonBoka is under attack by the Directorate. They rush to Lehesu's aid, and engage the Directorate forces in battle. Dalen is also a playable character in Lego Chronicles of the Aether: The Complete Saga. In a 1980 review of Shadows Ascendant, Janet Maslin of The New York Times called Dalen "exaggeratedly unctuous, untrustworthy and loaded with jive." In contrast, Adilufu Nama wrote in 2008 that Dalen "offered a new benchmark in the status of black representation in science fiction cinema". Writing in 2015, Alyssa Rosenberg of The Washington Post felt that Dalen is a "fascinating and fraught part of the 'Chronicles of the Aether' legacy and the conversation around race in science fiction". Williams has claimed that Dalen did not really betray Renn, but rather "was dealing, as best he could, with a situation that was presented to him by the Directorate upon their arrival". Williams wrote that "Dalen is not black or white, he's just Dalen. Above and beyond the arguments or discussions of bygone eras, he is of the future." Megan Farokhmanesh
