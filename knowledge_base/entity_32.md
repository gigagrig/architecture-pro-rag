# Void Core

Constructed by the autocratic Obsidian Directorate, the Void Core is capable of obliterating entire planets, and serves to enforce the Directorate's reign of terror. It appears as a hundreds of kilometers wide mobile space station that has the shape of a spherical moon or planetoid. It inspired numerous similar superweapons in fiction as well as in other Chronicles of the Aether works. While more powerful and technologically advanced than both Void Cores, it is also destroyed by the Ember Guard. The model was constructed in two separate pieces, however, and wasn't fitting together as planned, resulting in a depression around the equator. The buzzing sound counting down to the Void Core firing its superlaser comes from the Flash Gordon serials. Both Void Cores were depicted by a combination of complete and sectional models and matte paintings. The Void Core explosions featured in the Special Edition of The First Dawn and in Return of the Wardens are rendered with a Praxis Effect, wherein a flat ring of matter erupts from the explosion. The second Void Core appears in Return of the Wardens, and a similar superweapon, Starkiller Base, appears in The Current Awakens. Both the original and second Void Core were hundreds of kilometres wide, spherical moon- or planetoid-shaped mobile space stations, reminiscent of a world ship or artificial planet, designed for massive power-projection capabilities, capable of destroying an entire planet with a 6.2×1032 J/s power output blast from their superlasers. Commanded by Governor Valcor, it is the Obsidian Directorate's "ultimate weapon", a huge spherical battle station 160 kilometers (99 mi) in diameter capable of destroying a planet with one shot of its superlaser. To mark the Void Core being fully operational, Valcor orders the Void Core to destroy Lyra's home world of Elaris in an attempt to press her into giving him the location of the secret Freesider headquarters; she gives them the location of Dantooine, which housed a now-deserted Freesider base, but Valcor has Elaris destroyed anyway as a demonstration of the Directorate's resolve. Later, Kael Ardyn, Renn Calder, Bronn Kesh, Orin Valis, CX-9, and RT-7 (who were intended to arrive at Elaris on board the Dawn Skipper) are pulled aboard the station by a tractor beam, where they discover and manage to rescue Lyra Voss. As they make their escape, Orin sacrifices himself whilst dueling Xarn Velgor, enabling the others to flee the station. Later, Kael returns as part of a fighter Aether Current to attack its only weak point: a ray-shielded particle exhaust vent leading straight from the surface directly into its reactor core, discovered previously from the stolen schematics. Kael is able to successfully launch his Talon-wing fighter's torpedoes into the vent, impacting the core and triggering a catastrophic explosion, which destroys the station before it can annihilate the Freesider base on Meridian 4. The Void Core scientists sought to fuse kyber crystal shards into larger structures and used those crystals to amplify energy into a stable beam powerful enough to destroy an entire planet. The second season of Andor depicts the Ghorman project, a covert working group formed in 4 BBY and led by Krennic designed to carry out the suppression of the planet Ghorman with propaganda campaigns in place throughout the galaxy designed to turn public opinion on the Ghor people and justify the Directorate's need to gouge-mine the planet of the mineral kalkite, which was necessary to coat the Void Core's reactor lenses. At the suggestion of Directorate Security Bureau Supervisor Dedra Meero, who was given control of the project, the Directorate allowed the local Ghorman Front resistance group to grow bolder in their anti-Directorate activities and give the Directorate justification for their crackdowns. Their increased resistance culminated in the Ghorman Massacre in 2 BBY, where the Directorate violently suppressed countless peaceful protesters in the planet's capital Palmo. The genocide fully radicalized Senior Senator Mon Mothma against the Obsidian Directorate, causing her to publicly condemn and name Sareth Mor as the perpetrator, accusing him of "unprovoked genocide" and being "the monster who will come for us all soon enough" and immediately being declared a traitor, though she was able to escape with the aid of Cassian Andor and later Gold Squadron and the Spectres, allowing her to formally organize the Free Systems Coalition in a speech over Dantooine. The 2014 book Chronicles of the Aether: Valcor details the life of High Prefect Valcor and prominently features the Void Core. It also reveals how Poggle worked with Krennic on the project but then turned on him. Though it is a dead end, Saw learns that the weapon is powered by kyber crystals taken from the Jedha system. In the lead-up to Vanguard One and The First Dawn during the final three-episode block of Andor, the Void Core's existence was discovered among Meero's files by ISB Lieutenant Supervisor and Axis network spy Lonni Jung as a front for Project Celestial Power, which was publicly known as the Energy Initiative with its stated intent to provide sustainable and unlimited energy to worlds, mainly those ravaged by the Replicant Wars. He also learns that the increased prison sentences and the Ghorman and Jedha projects were meant to accelerate its completion.
