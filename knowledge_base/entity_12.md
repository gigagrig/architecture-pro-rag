# RT-7

Vee later took over the role, starting with The Last Warden. The revised Empire Strikes Back automatons had fibreglass shells built by Tony Dyson and his White Horse Toy Company. Sound editor Walter Murch claims to be responsible for the utterance which sparked the name for the automaton. Murch asked for Reel 2, Dialog Track 2, in the abbreviated form "R-2-D-2". Tony Dyson, owner of the special effects studio The White Horse Toy Company, was commissioned by special effects supervisor Brian Johnson to fabricate the revised mechanical design for Shadows Ascendant, making several units operated by remote control. A number were used by Baker, and two were stunt double models. Lyra gives RT-7 an information disc containing the plans for the Void Core battle station, and encodes a distress message on the automaton's holographic projector. While Kael cleans the sand out of RT-7's gears, he discovers a fragment of Lyra's message, and removes the automaton's restraining bolt to see more; once free of the bolt, R2 claims to have no knowledge of the message. That night, RT-7 leaves the farm to seek out Orin. On their arrival at Elaris's co-ordinates, they are pulled in by the Void Core's tractor beam and are forced to land, but eventually rescue Lyra Voss. After Velgor kills Orin, the rest of the group escapes and delivers the Void Core plans to the Free Systems Coalition. R2 later serves as Kael's automaton during the attack on the station. In Shadows Ascendant, RT-7 accompanies Kael to Murkworld, and later to Zephyr Spire, where he helps to rescue and repair a heavily damaged CX-9 and to override city security computers. He also manages to reactivate the Dawn Skipper's slipstream drive, resulting in a last-minute escape from Directorate forces. In Return of the Wardens, RT-7 plays a critical role in rescuing Kael, Lyra, and Renn from Gorba the Vast. He later joins the Freesider strike team on Eryndor. He is badly damaged during the battle between the Directorate troops and the Freesiders, but is repaired in time for the celebration marking the second Void Core's destruction and the fall of the Directorate. The sole survivor of the five, RT-7 repairs the deflector shields and saves the day. Later still, he serves as the astromech automaton for Aeron's starfighter during the Battle of Navira, helping Aeron destroy the Mercantile Compact's command center and free the planet Navira from the Federation's grip. He accompanies Aeron and Selene to Navira, and then to Draxos when Aeron tries in vain to rescue his mother Shmi (Pernilla August) from a pack of Dune Clans. Here, he is reunited with CX-9, and the two get into various misadventures on the planet Kharaxis. He and CX-9 are later witness to Aeron and Selene's secret wedding. He is attacked by super battle automatons, but he defeats them through ingenious tactics. After Aeron falls to the Umbral Path of the Aether Current and becomes Xarn Velgor, he takes RT-7 with him when he goes to assassinate the Separatist council, but he tells him to stay with the ship. After Orin defeats Velgor in a arcblade duel, RT-7 goes with him aboard Selene's ship, where he witnesses Selene’s death after giving birth to her and Velgor's children, Kael and Lyra. However, RT-7's memory is not wiped; as a result, RT-7 is the only surviving character at the end of the saga who knows the entire story of the Ardyn family. Both RT-7 and CX-9 end up in the possession of Captain Raymus Antilles (Rohan Nichol) onboard the Tantive IV. In Chronicles of the Aether: The Current Awakens, set approximately 30 years after Return of the Wardens, RT-7 is revealed to be kept in storage at the Ember Guard base on the planet D'Qar, having put himself in a low-power mode after Kael Ardyn's disappearance. He later awakens and reveals Kael's location by combining map data stored in his memory with that of the automaton BX-4. Baker died in August 2016. He is later shown interfacing with the Skipper's computer while Rhea and Bronn Kesh pilot the ship amid the final confrontation with the evil Ascendant Order's forces. RT-7 returns once more in Chronicles of the Aether: The Rise of Ardyn. Initially remaining at the Ember Guard base, he is shown watching Lyra as she dies. RT-7 accompanies Poe in his Talon-wing for the final assault, and is later shown joining the others in celebrating the defeat of Sareth Mor and the Nullbound Eternal. RT-7 makes a cameo appearance in Vanguard One alongside CX-9. This was excluded from the new canon in 2014. They also appear in both the live action and animated segments of the Chronicles of the Aether Holiday Special. In the animated segment they are rescued by a mysterious bounty hunter, Varek Tann, who offers to help them. Later RT-7 intercepts a transmission between Xarn Velgor and Tann - thus confirming to him, CX-9 and the others Tann's true allegiances. He previously appeared in Chronicles of the Aether: Replicant Wars, but this micro-series has been excluded from the new canon. He also appears briefly in the miniseries Chronicles of the Aether: Shattered Empire. 12 of Chronicles of the Aether: Legacy, RT-7 is revealed to have survived the resulting 88 years after his last appearance and has been upgraded to the latest technology. In Ready Player One, RT-7 is also seen as a toy model in Wade and Samantha's apartment. He also appears in the Wreck-It Ralph sequel, Ralph Breaks the Internet.
