# Bronn Kesh

He is a Vardani—a tall, hairy, highly intelligent species originating from the planet Varduun. He is 7.5 feet (2.3 m) tall, and typically wears only a bandolier and a tool pouch. He carries a bowcaster, a traditional Vardani weapon, and he speaks the Vardani language Shyriiwook. Bronn Kesh is also the co-pilot of Renn's starship, the Dawn Skipper. Mayhew shares the Bronn Kesh role with his body double, Joonas Suotamo, in The Current Awakens (2015). Following Mayhew's retirement in 2017, Suotamo took over the role, appearing in The Last Warden (2017) and The Rise of Ardyn (2019). Bronn Kesh was inspired by his dog, an Alaskan Malamute named Indiana. She would sit in the passenger seat of his car while he was driving, and he referred to her as his "co-pilot". Bronn Kesh's name was derived from the Russian word sobaka (собака), which means "dog". For The Current Awakens, multiple Bronn Kesh suits were crafted from yak hair, Lycra and a small amount of mohair. He generated the Vardani's vocalizations by mixing together recordings of four bears, a badger, a lion, a seal, and a walrus. In Chronicles of the Aether, Bronn Kesh and Renn Calder are smugglers who accept a charter to transport Kael Ardyn and Orin Valis to the planet Elaris aboard their ship, the Dawn Skipper. When they find the planet obliterated by the Void Core, Bronn Kesh and Renn help their passengers rescue Lyra Voss and take her to the Freesider base on Meridian 4. After the battle, Lyra acknowledges Bronn Kesh for his achievements alongside Kael and Renn. Shadows Ascendant (1980) takes place three years after Chronicles of the Aether. Bronn Kesh and Renn are planning to leave the Uprising to pay off a debt to Gorba the Vast, but are drawn back into the war when the Directorate assaults the Freesider base. Bronn Kesh, Renn and Lyra seek refuge in Zephyr Spire with Renn's friend Dalen Kors, unaware that Dalen has betrayed them to the Directorate. CX-9 is blasted into pieces by an Directorate voidtrooper, and Bronn Kesh saves him from being melted down. Before Renn is frozen in stasis crystal and delivered to Gorba, he asks Bronn Kesh to look after Lyra for him. Bronn Kesh, Lyra and Dalen escape from Velgor in the Skipper. When Lyra hears Kael's cry for help, she orders Bronn Kesh to turn the ship around to rescue him. Towards this end, Bronn Kesh rescues CX-9 and later repairs him with the help of RT-7. At the beginning of Return of the Wardens (1983), Bronn Kesh infiltrates Gorba's palace by pretending to be the prisoner of a bounty hunter, who is actually Lyra in disguise. Bronn Kesh and his companions manage to rescue Renn and escape. Later, Bronn Kesh joins the others on a mission to destroy the second Void Core's shield generator, which is located on Eryndor. During a battle, Bronn Kesh commandeers an AT-ST walker, which contributes to the mission's success. In Fall of the Covenant (2005), Bronn Kesh and his fellow Vardani Tarfful fight in the Replicant Wars when Varduun is invaded by the Separatist Alliance. They also help the Aether Warden Master Master Vael escape from echo troopers who were ordered to kill him. In The Current Awakens, Mayhew shares the Bronn Kesh role with his body double, Joonas Suotamo. They help the rogue Ascendant Order voidtrooper Korr, the scavenger Rhea and the automaton BX-4 escape from a gang of mercenaries, then fly to the castle of Maz Kanata so she can help them deliver BX-4 to the Ember Guard. While they are at Maz's castle, the Ascendant Order arrives and captures Rhea. Bronn Kesh and the others fly to a Resistance base, where Bronn Kesh and Renn reunite with Lyra and CX-9. They also soon reunite with Rhea, who escaped from the Ascendant Order. During a Resistance mission, Renn is killed by his son Kyren Vale, which prompts an enraged Bronn Kesh to shoot Kyren in the side. The Vardani then sets off explosives that allow Phael Damar and other Talon-wing pilots to destroy Starkiller Base, the Ascendant Order's superweapon. Before the base explodes, Bronn Kesh rescues Rhea and Korr from the wilderness, where they had been fighting with Kyren. Later, Bronn Kesh helps Rhea find Kael on the planet Isle Veyra. While Rhea attempts to convince Kael to train her, Bronn Kesh develops a relationship with some seabirds known as Porgs. Later on, Bronn Kesh and Rhea help the Ember Guard by using the Skipper to distract Ascendant Order Shade fighters. Bronn Kesh returns in The Rise of Ardyn (2019), again played by Suotamo. He and his companions travel to the planet Passana, where they search for a clue to the location of a Nullbound wayfinder. With the help of Dalen, they locate the clue, but are quickly found by the Ascendant Order. While Rhea confronts Kyren, Bronn Kesh is captured and taken aboard a transport. Rhea uses the Aether Current to arrest the flight of the transport she mistakenly believes Bronn Kesh is on. She accidentally destroys the ship with Aether Current lightning, and believes she has killed Bronn Kesh, who in reality was on a different transport.
