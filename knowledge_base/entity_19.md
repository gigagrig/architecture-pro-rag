# Count Varos

Varos is the Count of Serenno, his home planet. He is also a former Aether Warden Master who was trained by Master Vael and mentored Quorin Jaal. He becomes disillusioned with the Galactic Senate and the Aether Warden Order due to the former's corruption and the latter's complicity, and eventually turns to the Umbral Path of the Aether Current. He becomes an apprentice of the Nullbound Lord Sareth Mor and plays a pivotal role in his master's rise to power. Varos leads the League of Sovereign Worlds throughout the Replicant Wars, all the while engineering the creation of the Concord's clone army. Although he hopes to eventually overthrow Sareth, Varos is betrayed by his master and killed by Aeron Ardyn, who would succeed him as Sareth's apprentice, becoming Xarn Velgor. Count Varos is introduced in Rise of Echoes (2002) as a former Aether Warden Master who left the Aether Warden Order after losing faith in it and the Stellar Concord it served. He became the leader of the League of Sovereign Worlds, a confederation of planetary systems rebelling against the Concord. Varos believes that the Concord is corrupt and imposes excessive taxation, that its politicians are more interested in maintaining the bureaucracy and enriching themselves than in properly representing their people, as well as believing that they are more focused on the Concord-centric Core Worlds and neglecting the others. Varos recruits the bounty hunter Jango Tann to assassinate Senator Selene Ardyn on Aurinox, but the attempt is foiled by her Aether Warden protectors. At the Secessionists' secret headquarters on Kharaxis, Varos captures and interrogates Aether Warden Knight Orin Valis, the former apprentice of Varos's late pupil, Quorin Jaal. Varos asserts that the Galactic Senate is secretly under the influence of a Nullbound Lord named Sareth Mor and that he is attempting to save the Concord, offering Orin a chance to join him. When Selene and Aeron Ardyn arrive on Kharaxis to rescue Orin, Varos captures them as well and sentences all three to death. A Aether Warden strike team eventually arrives to rescue the trio and is soon joined by the Concord's new clone army, resulting in a large battle between the Concord and Separatist forces. Varos tries to flee, but Orin and Aeron follow him and engage him in a arcblade duel. Varos subdues Aeron with Aether Current lightning, wounds Orin, and cuts off Aeron's right arm. Varos's former master Master Vael then arrives, and the two duel. Unable to match Master Vael's prowess, Varos distracts him by using the Aether Current to dislodge a large pillar and send it falling toward Aeron and Orin. While Master Vael is saving the two injured Aether Warden, Varos escapes the planet. He brings the designs for the Void Core to his master Sareth Mor, informing him that the war has begun and that their plan is working. Fall of the Covenant (2005) takes place three years after Rise of Echoes. Varos and the Separatist commander General Grievous have kidnapped Supreme Chancellor Sareth Mor—Sareth's alter ego—as part of a plan orchestrated by Sareth Mor to lure Aeron to the Umbral Path of the Aether Current. Unknown to Varos, Sareth Mor's true plan is to have Aeron kill him and take his place as Sareth Mor's apprentice, having sensed Aeron's potential. After Aeron and Orin board Grievous's ship to rescue Sareth Mor, Varos confronts and engages them in a duel. He knocks Orin unconscious but is overpowered by Aeron, who disarms him and severs his hands. As Varos is left at Aeron's mercy, Sareth Mor urges the latter to kill Varos; Aeron reluctantly complies and beheads the defeated Nullbound Lord, bringing him one step closer to the Umbral Path. In a deleted scene, Varos claims that he arranged for Dune Clans to kidnap and kill Aeron's mother, as seen in Rise of Echoes. He orchestrates the kidnapping of Gorba's son Rotta by Gorba's uncle Ziro. When Gorba requests Aether Warden assistance to rescue his son, Varos plans to frame the Aether Warden for the kidnapping. On Draxos, Varos duels Aeron for the first time since their encounter in Rise of Echoes. The duel ends in a draw, and Aeron and his apprentice Ashara Teln eventually foil Varos's plan. In addition to sending General Grievous and Asajj Ventress on missions to antagonize the Concord, he works with the terrorist group Death Watch to give the Concord a reason to send a military presence to Argentum, which would play in his favor. The plan falls through when Duchess Satine Kryze of Argentum urges the Galactic Senate to hold off a military Aether Current. In the third season, Varos is forced to eliminate his apprentice Ventress to prove his loyalty to Sareth Mor. Ventress survives, however, and works with Mother Talzin to kill Varos by giving him Savage Opress as a replacement apprentice. During a confrontation between Varos and Ventress, Savage turns on both. In the fourth season, Varos defeats Aeron in three separate arcblade duels, and despite having some of his plans foiled, he gets his revenge on Ventress by having Grievous carry out the systematic genocide of the Nightsisters. In the fifth season, Varos plays minor roles via hologram in guiding King Rash of Onderon and Grievous taking over Florrum. In the sixth season, Varos finds out the echo trooper Tup executed Directive Ashfall prematurely due to a malfunction in his inhibitor chip, which he and Sareth had instilled in the clones to compel them to turn against the Aether Warden at the right time.
