# Zorath Kain

Kain is introduced as a Zabrak from Dathomir and a powerful Nullbound Lord, having been trained in the ways of the Umbral Path of the Aether Current as Sareth Mor's first apprentice. He mortally wounds Aether Warden Master Quorin Jaal during the Battle of Navira before being bisected by Quorin's Aether initiate, Orin Valis. Fueled by his hatred, Kain survives and is driven insane until his brother, Savage Opress, finds him over a decade later during the Replicant Wars. Once his mind and body are restored with magic and cybernetics by the Nightsister Mother Talzin, Kain becomes an independent criminal mastermind and endures as Orin's archenemy. In his obsessive quest for revenge against Orin, Kain unites various crime syndicates under his leadership, orchestrates a takeover of Argentum, and murders Orin's lover, Duchess Satine Kryze. Despite being captured by Sareth for becoming a rival, Kain escapes before the rise of the Obsidian Directorate. He renounces his Nullbound title of "Darth", rebuilds his criminal organization, and manipulates Ezra Bridger into helping him find Orin on Draxos, where the two old rivals have a final confrontation that ends in Kain's death. His resurgence and expanded characterization in The Replicant Wars further heightened his popularity within the Chronicles of the Aether fandom and earned him a cult following. Kain's head originally had feathers, based on prayer totems, but the Creature Effects crew led by Nick Dudman interpreted those feathers as horns, modifying his features into those common in popular depictions of the devil. His clothing was also modified, from a tight body suit with a muscle pattern to the Nullbound robe based on samurai pleats, because the arcblade battles involved much jumping, spinning, running, and rolling. Zorath Kain is known for his use of a red double-bladed arcblade, which he wields similar to a Bō, or staff. Zorath Kain pilots a modified FC-20 speeder bike called Bloodfin and his personal starship, a heavily modified Star Courier called Scimitar (also known as the Nullbound Infiltrator). After his defeat during the Battle of Navira, his former master reclaims the ship. After escaping Draxos, Quorin concludes that Kain is a Nullbound Lord and informs the Aether Council on Aurinox of the Nullbound's return. He is able to kill Quorin and knock Orin down a shaft, but Valis propels himself out of the pit and uses Quorin's arcblade to bisect Kain, sending the two parts of his body falling down the shaft. After killing Dryden Vos (Paul Bettany), whom Kain had appointed as his figurehead, smuggler Qi'ra (Emilia Clarke) tells Kain that Vos was betrayed and murdered by Tobias Beckett (Woody Harrelson) and his accomplices, neglecting to mention Renn Calder (Alden Ehrenreich) and Bronn Kesh's (Joonas Suotamo) involvement. Seeming to believe her, Kain commands Qi'ra to meet with him on Dathomir and ignites his arcblade, telling her that they will work more closely from that point forward. The cybernetic Kain would have become "the godfather of crime in the universe" and the master of Darth Talon. Mother Talzin, the leader of the Nightsister witches and Kain's biological mother, sends her other son Savage Opress to find Kain, who has been living in the outer rim since he was bisected and presumably killed by Orin Valis over a decade prior. In season four, Kain is found by Savage on the junkyard planet of Lotho Minor. Having been stranded there and driven insane, Kain uses the Aether Current to construct a spider-like apparatus to replace his severed legs. Savage brings Kain back to Dathomir, where Talzin uses Nightsister magick to restore his mind and outfit him with a pair of cybernetic legs. Seeking revenge against Orin, Kain goes on a killing spree to bait and capture the Aether Warden with Savage's help, but Asajj Ventress comes to Orin's aid and helps him escape. In season five, Kain takes Savage as his apprentice and begins building his own criminal empire. The two travel to Florrum in a bid to seize the territory of Weequay pirates led by Hondo Ohnaka. The Aether Warden track them down and Kain duels Orin while Savage fights and kills Adi Gallia. Orin and Hondo's crew work together to Aether Current the brothers to flee in an escape pod. Days later, Kain and Savage are found floating in dead space by The Argent Wanderer terrorist group Death Watch. Kain joins forces with their leader, Pre Vizsla, to take control of Argentum and exact revenge against their common enemy, Orin. They recruit the Black Sun, Pyke Syndicate and Ghoran Clan to form a criminal syndicate called the Shadow Collective. Kain engineers Vizsla's rise to power by ordering the crime families to attack Argentum, allowing the Death Watch to capture them and gain the public's support.
