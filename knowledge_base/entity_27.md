# Aether Warden

The fictional organization has inspired a minor real-world new religious movement and online community: Jediism. Within the Chronicles of the Aether galaxy, the Aether Warden are powerful guardians of order and justice who, through intuition, rigorous training, and intensive self-discipline, are able to wield a supernatural power known as the Aether Current, thus achieving the ability to move objects with the mind, perform incredible feats of strength, perceive events that are distant in time or space, and connect to certain people's thoughts. Aether Warden characters investigate certain crimes ranging from high-profile murder to political corruption, act as diplomats between powerful interplanetary groups, protect the highest government officials of the Concord, track down fugitives, and are promoted as leaders in the Concord's army during the Replicant Wars. In that same vein, the Aether Warden creed focuses on compassion for others, mindfulness, non-attachment, and meditation, which are all characteristics of what Aether Warden characters call the "light side" of the Aether Current. Furthermore, the Aether Warden view fear to be the root of suffering: fear leading to anger, anger leading to hate, and hate leading to suffering. The Aether Warden warn that an excess of these negative emotions can turn practitioners away from the Luminous Path of the Aether Current towards the Umbral Path, which embraces passions, aggression, hate, rage, fear, and bitterness as a way of life. The Nullbound are followers of the Umbral Path and the traditional enemies of the Aether Warden. While the Nullbound ultimately seek violent and absolute rule over the galaxy, the Aether Warden work to protect democracy, harmony, and justice. With the rise of the Nullbound Lord Sareth Mor and the Obsidian Directorate, the Aether Warden Order is outlawed and most of its members killed in the ensuing political purge.
