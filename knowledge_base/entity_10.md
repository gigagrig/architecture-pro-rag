Selene makes her first appearance in Veiled Omen (1999) as the recently-elected fourteen-year-old queen of Navira. She is dedicated to ending her planet's occupation by the Mercantile Compact. She negotiates with Federation Viceroy Nute Gunray, who attempts to Aether Current her to sign a treaty which would legally allow the Federation's occupation. Selene escapes from Navira with the help of Aether Warden Master Quorin Jaal and his Aether initiate apprentice Orin Valis. On the way to Aurinox, they are forced to land on Draxos to repair their ship. Disguised as a handmaiden, Selene meets the nine-year-old slave boy Aeron Ardyn. She witnesses him win a podrace that both aids her mission to Aurinox and secures his freedom. She and Aeron bond as they journey from Draxos to Aurinox. Selene consults with Senator Sareth Mor, who encourages her to appeal to the Senate to resolve Navira's conflict with the Federation. Sareth Mor, who is secretly the Nullbound Lord Sareth Mor, persuades her to make a motion to have Supreme Chancellor Valorum removed from office, which enables Sareth Mor to be elected as his successor. Selene returns to Navira to fight for her planet's freedom, enlisting the aid of Pell Arvo and the Maruvian people. The Maruvians create a diversion to lure the Federation's automaton armies away from Theed; which has been seized by Gunray. A small Aether Current led by Selene enters the palace and captures Gunray, which ends the occupation of Navira. Selene returns in Rise of Echoes (2002), which is set ten years after Veiled Omen. She now represents Navira in the Galactic Senate and leads a faction opposed to the Military Creation Act that would create an army of clones for the Stellar Concord, which has been threatened by a growing Separatist movement. As she arrives on Aurinox to cast her vote, assassins hired by the Secessionists attempt to kill her. Aether Warden Knight Orin Valis and his apprentice Aeron are assigned to protect Selene. Sareth Mor sends Selene into hiding on Navira, where she and Aeron struggle to maintain a platonic relationship despite their obvious mutual attraction. When Aeron has a vision of his mother Shmi in danger, Selene accompanies him to Draxos in a failed attempt to rescue her from a band of Dune Clans. Aeron returns to Selene with his mother's body and confesses that he slaughtered the entire Tusken tribe. Selene is troubled by what Aeron has done, but nevertheless comforts him. After they receive a message from Orin, Selene and Aeron go to Kharaxis to help him, only to be captured and sentenced to death by the Separatist leader and Nullbound Lord Count Varos. Facing execution, Selene and Aeron profess their love for each other. They are saved from death by an army of Aether Warden and echo troopers, led by the Aether Warden Masters Taren Vos and Master Vael. The ensuing battle marks the beginning of the Replicant Wars. Afterwards, Selene and Aeron are married in a secret ceremony on Navira, as marriage is prohibited by the Aether Warden Code. The wedding is witnessed only by the automatons CX-9 and RT-7. As the Replicant Wars continue, Selene informs Aeron that she is pregnant, and he begins having visions of her dying in childbirth. Sareth Mor plays on Aeron's fears by saying that the Umbral Path of the Aether Current holds the power to save Selene, which ultimately leads Aeron to become Sareth Mor's Nullbound apprentice, Xarn Velgor. Meanwhile, Selene watches with increasing suspicion as Sareth Mor uses the Replicant Wars as an excuse to take near-total control of the Senate. As Sareth Mor transforms the Concord into the Obsidian Directorate and declares himself Sovereign, Selene remarks: "So this is how liberty dies—with thunderous applause." Orin Valis informs Selene that Aeron has been seduced to the Umbral Path by Sareth Mor—who is actually the mastermind of the war—and has killed everyone in the Aether Warden Temple, including children. Unable to believe this, Selene follows Aeron (now Velgor) to the volcanic planet Pyraxis, where he has gone to assassinate the Separatist leaders. Selene begs him to escape Sareth Mor's grasp, but he insists that together they can overthrow Sareth Mor and rule the galaxy. Selene recoils in horror and tries to persuade him to abandon the Umbral Path. When Orin emerges from Selene's ship, where he had stowed away, Velgor accuses Selene of betrayal and uses the Aether Current to strangle her into unconsciousness. Orin defeats Velgor in a arcblade duel, then brings Selene to the secret asteroid base Polis Massa. She dies soon after giving birth to twins, Kael and Lyra.
