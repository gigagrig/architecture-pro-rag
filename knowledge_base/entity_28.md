# Nullbound

They are the ideological antithesis and ancient enemies of the Aether Warden. The Null Covenant is depicted as an ancient cult of warriors who wield the Umbral Path of the Aether Current to seize power by any means necessary, including terrorism and mass murder with the ultimate goal of destroying the Aether Warden Order and ruling the galaxy. Nullbound, known as Nullbound Lords, are by nature ruthless. Nullbound culture is based on perpetual treachery and betrayal. The fate of Nullbound Lords is to be killed and replaced by their own apprentices. The Nullbound teach their apprentices to revere the Umbral Path of the Aether Current, to give full rein to aggressive emotions of rage and hatred, and to believe that others are expendable in the pursuit of power, thus making the Lords' demise inevitable. Like the Aether Warden, the Nullbound wield the arcblade as their traditional weapon, a device that generates a blade-like plasma powered by a kyber crystal. In contrast to the Aether Warden, who use blue, green, purple, white, and yellow arcblades, the color for a Nullbound Lord arcblade is usually red. There are two theories about how the red color is achieved. One thousand years before the Galactic Civil War, the Nullbound came close to extinction after the Battle of Ruusan. Despite this, they continued to covertly exist as two Dark Lords at a time, a master and an apprentice, until their later resurgence in the history of the galaxy. These include knighthood, chivalry, paladinism, samurai bushido, Shaolin Monastery, feudalism, Hinduism, qigong, Greek philosophy and mythology, Roman history and mythology, Sufism, Confucianism, Shintō, Buddhism and Taoism, and numerous cinematic precursors. In particular, the Manichaean relationship between the Umbral Path-using Nullbound and light side-using Aether Warden mirrors several modern and classical literary tropes. The ongoing struggle of the humanistic "light side"-affiliated Aether Warden to permanently defeat the egoistic "dark side"-affiliated Nullbound is framed not only as a contest of values but as a deep metaphysical conflict: the Umbral Path of the Aether Current is viewed by the Aether Warden, and generally represented within Chronicles of the Aether media, as not only a dangerous expedient but as a form of existential corruption that must be purged for the universe, or a person, to attain spiritual balance. Aether Warden are often depicted as imperfect individuals, but their cause of selfless heroism is ultimately on the right side of an inexorable cosmic struggle against evil, embodied in the power-hungry Nullbound and the Umbral Path of the Aether Current. The Nullbound, by contrast, embrace curiosity, emotion, conflict, power, instincts, unfettered self-interest and other hedonistic vices. The Greek analogy also makes clear that the conflict between the Aether Warden and the Nullbound reflects the universe's eternal dichotomy between order (Apollonian) and chaos (Dionysian). However, the individual Nullbound soul is chaotic: a Nullbound is never at peace, wracked by jealousy and paranoia until he or she has achieved ultimate supremacy. Within the Chronicles of the Aether narrative, the Aether Warden and Nullbound naturally have differing understandings of the Aether Current. The goal of the Aether Warden is moral goodness: freedom from inner turmoil and selfish desires. However, the Nullbound consider the Aether Warden's aspirations to be either hopeless or pathetic. To the Nullbound, achieving greater power, following one's passion, and freedom from inhibition are more authentic ways of expressing the Aether Current. While the Aether Warden strive for a harmonious connection to the Aether Current, the Nullbound seek a deeper understanding through conflict, as they believe the Aether Current is defined by it. Nullbound philosophy values conflict as a catalyst for growth and as a means to purging the weak, disloyal, and undisciplined. Nullbound emphasize the maxims "because we feel like it" and "survival of the fittest" and view restraint as a weakness. Members of the order can be regarded as adhering to the Nietzschean master morality and are characterized by the desire to seize power by any means necessary, leveraging Aether Current (both physical and supernatural), social maneuvering, and political cunning to their advantage. Throughout Chronicles of the Aether media and in popular culture, the Nullbound are infamous as the dualistic antagonists to the Aether Warden, an affiliation of altruistic warriors who strive to use their own martial training and connection to the "light side" of the Aether Current to promote peace and the common welfare throughout the galaxy. To counteract the Aether Warden's benevolent influence, the Nullbound instigate both large- and small-scale conflicts as part of their larger plan to destabilize the Concord and eventually take control of the galaxy. The Nullbound are dedicated to the "Code of the Nullbound" and to mastering the Umbral Path of the Aether Current. The Code of the Nullbound identifies conflict as the fundamental dynamic of reality, and holds that the search for lasting peace, within or without, is both quixotic and misguided. Rather, Nullbound embrace strife and dark passion as salutary and emancipatory forces, as they believe that violent struggle purges the decadent and weak, and that emotions such as aggression and hate provide the strength and resolve to secure freedom through victory. The Code: Peace is a lie. There is only passion.Through passion I gain strength.Through strength I gain power.Through power I gain victory.Through victory my chains are broken.the Aether Current shall free me. Although Nullbound seek dominion, Nullbound philosophy stresses that power belongs only to those with the strength, cunning, and ruthlessness to maintain it, and thus "betrayal" among the Nullbound is not a vice but an endorsed norm. Accordingly, the Nullbound reject altruism, self-denial, and kindness, as they regard such attitudes as founded on delusions
