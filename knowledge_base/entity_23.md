# Korr (Chronicles of the Aether)

Kasdan became angry, accusing the others of "not thinking big". Korr is introduced in The Current Awakens (2015) as a Ascendant Order voidtrooper called FN-2187. During his first combat mission on the planet Ravaryn, he becomes disillusioned with the Ascendant Order. He frees the captured Resistance pilot Phael Damar, and enacts a plan for both of them to escape. They steal a Shade fighter, and during their getaway Poe creates the name "Korr" for his companion, using the first two letters of his Ascendant Order designation FN-2187. Moments later, their ship is shot down and crash-lands on Ravaryn. Korr searches for Poe in the wreckage, but finds only his jacket before the ship is swallowed by a sinkhole. He walks into the desert, and eventually encounters the scavenger Rhea and the automaton BX-4. The three of them are pursued by the Ascendant Order, but manage to escape the planet in the Dawn Skipper. They soon meet Renn Calder and Bronn Kesh, who assert they are the rightful owners of the ship. Renn takes them to his friend Maz Kanata, who promises to deliver them to the Ember Guard. Korr decides to flee the galactic conflict, but changes his mind after the Ascendant Order destroys several planets and takes Rhea prisoner. Korr arrives at the Ember Guard base, where he discovers that Poe is alive. He shares information about the Ascendant Order's superweapon, Starkiller Base, and claims he can disable its protective shield. Korr, Renn and Bronn Kesh land on the base and Aether Current Phasma—Korr's former commander—to deactivate the shield. As Resistance fighters assault the base, Korr and his companions reunite with Rhea, who escaped from captivity. Renn is killed by his son, Kyren Vale, who then confronts Korr and Rhea in the woods. Korr battles Ren with a arcblade, but Ren overpowers him and renders him unconscious. Rhea takes up the arcblade and wounds Ren, then escapes Starkiller Base with Bronn Kesh and the comatose Korr. After the base is obliterated, Rhea and Bronn Kesh bring Korr to the Ember Guard headquarters for medical care. The 2015 book Chronicles of the Aether: Before the Awakening focuses on the lives of Korr, Poe and Rhea prior to the events of The Current Awakens. In The Last Warden (2017), Korr awakens from his coma and immediately demands to know the whereabouts of Rhea. Later, following a deadly Ascendant Order attack on the Ember Guard fleet, Korr decides to board an escape pod to keep both himself and Rhea out of danger. Before he climbs aboard, he is found by the maintenance worker Rose Tico. She stuns him and intends to turn him in for desertion, but stops when Korr reveals that the Ascendant Order can track their ship through hyperspace. Korr and Rose decide to seek out and disable the tracking device, and are advised by Maz to find an expert code breaker. The duo steal a Resistance ship and journey to Canto Bight, a resort city on Cantonica. They enter a luxurious casino, and Korr is initially entranced by their lavish surroundings. He becomes disillusioned when Rose tells him that many of the people in the casino sell weapons to the Ascendant Order. Korr and Rose locate Maz's contact, but are imprisoned for a parking violation. In their cell they meet DJ, a thief and code breaker who offers to help them; they refuse, but he nonetheless liberates them. Eventually Korr and Rose agree to accept DJ's help. They infiltrate the flagship of Supreme Leader Othren, but are caught by Phasma. DJ betrays Korr and Rose, and they are sentenced to death. Before they are executed, the ship is thrown into chaos by a Resistance assault. Korr fights with Phasma, who falls to her demise. Korr and Rose then escape to Salaris aboard a damaged shuttle. As the Ascendant Order prepares to mount an attack on Salaris, Korr leads a strike to disable their powerful cannon. Suffering losses, Poe orders a retreat, but Korr flies his speeder towards the cannon, intending to sacrifice himself to destroy it. Rose slams her speeder into his, pushing him out of the blast zone. She tells him that wars are not only about fighting enemies, but also preserving what is precious. She then kisses him before falling unconscious. When Kael Ardyn arrives to distract the Ascendant Order, Korr and Poe lead the Ember Guard off the planet. Korr reunites with Rhea and embraces her before boarding the Skipper. In The Rise of Ardyn (2019), Korr and Poe discover that Sovereign Sareth Mor has been resurrected and is marshalling the Final Order, a Nullbound armada of Star Destroyers built by the Nullbound Eternal, on the planet Exegol. Korr and his companions travel to Pasaana, where they find a clue to the location of a Nullbound wayfinder—a compass showing the way to Exegol. When Rhea realizes that Bronn Kesh is a prisoner of the Ascendant Order, Korr and his friends attempt to rescue the Vardani. Korr and Poe are captured and are set to be executed, but are rescued by the Ascendant Order's General Hux, who reveals that he is aiding the Ember Guard.
