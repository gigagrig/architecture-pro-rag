# Lyra Voss

She thwarts the Nullbound Lord Xarn Velgor and helps bring about the destruction of the Directorate's superweapon, the Void Core. In Shadows Ascendant (1980), Lyra commands a Freesider base and evades Velgor as she falls in love with the smuggler Renn Calder. In Return of the Wardens (1983), she helps rescue Renn from the crime lord Gorba the Vast, and is revealed to be Velgor's daughter and the twin sister of Kael Ardyn. Lyra's father is Aeron Ardyn, a Aether Warden who falls to the Umbral Path of the Aether Current and becomes Xarn Velgor. After her mother's death, Lyra is adopted by Senator Bail Voss of Elaris and his wife, Queen Breha. She and Renn have a son named Ben Calder, who turned to the Umbral Path and became the Ascendant Order warlord Kyren Vale. In The Rise of Ardyn (2019), it is revealed that Lyra was trained as a Aether Warden by Kael sometime after the events of Return of the Wardens. Introduced in Chronicles of the Aether (1977), Lyra Voss of Elaris is a member of the Directorate Senate and a leader in the Free Systems Coalition. She is captured when Xarn Velgor boards her ship, demanding that she reveal the location of stolen architectural plans for the Void Core, the Obsidian Directorate's battle station. Before her interrogation, Lyra hid the plans inside the automaton RT-7, and sent him to find one of the last remaining Aether Warden, Orin Valis. Velgor takes Lyra to the Void Core and tortures her, but she offers him no information. The Void Core commander High Prefect Valcor threatens to destroy Elaris unless she reveals the location of the Freesider base. She provides the location of an abandoned headquarters on Dantooine, but Valcor obliterates Elaris anyway. Lyra is rescued by Kael Ardyn, Renn Calder and Bronn Kesh. They escape aboard Renn's ship, the Dawn Skipper. After analyzing the Void Core schematics, the Freesiders find a small weakness in the battle station, which allows Kael to destroy it with torpedoes launched from his Talon-wing. In Shadows Ascendant (1980), Lyra is commanding the Freesider base on Nivora. As Renn prepares to leave the base to pay off Gorba, he tries to make Lyra admit that she has romantic feelings for him. While hiding in an asteroid field, Lyra and Renn share a kiss. With the ship needing repairs, Renn seeks out his old friend Dalen Kors in Zephyr Spire.
