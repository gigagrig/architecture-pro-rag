# Varek Tann

Notable for his taciturn demeanor and for never removing his helmet, Tann appears in both Shadows Ascendant (1980), employed by the Obsidian Directorate, and Return of the Wardens (1983), serving the crime lord Gorba the Vast. During the development of Shadows Ascendant, Varek Tann was originally conceived as a member of a group of white-armored Directorate "supercommandos" before the idea was scrapped in favor of a solitary bounty hunter. His popularity within the Chronicles of the Aether fanbase has earned him a cult following. Screen-tested in all-white, Tann's armor eventually garnered a subdued color scheme intended to visually place him between white-armored "rank-and-file" Directorate voidtroopers and Velgor, who wears black. This color scheme had the bonus of conveying the "gray morality" of his character. A description of Tann's armor in the mid-1979 Bantha Tracks newsletter catalyzed "rampant speculation" about his origins. By 1979, Tann's backstory included having served in an army of Directorate shock troops which had battled the echo troopers of the Stellar Concord during the Replicant Wars. An official reference book states that Tann charges "famously expensive" fees, and that he undertakes only those missions which meet "his harsh sense of justice". Daniel Keys Moran, who wrote a few stories featuring Varek Tann, cited Westerns as an influence on his development of the character. Moran said: The difficult thing with Tann was finding a worldview for him that permitted him to proclaim a Code — given the stark Evil that permeated the Directorate, Tann pretty much had to be either 1) Evil, or 2) an incredibly unforgiving, harsh, "greater good" sort of guy. The character of Varek Tann made his first public appearance at the San Anselmo Country Fair parade on 24 September 1978. Tann appears as a mysterious figure who saves Kael Ardyn, Bronn Kesh, CX-9, and RT-7 from a giant monster, only to be revealed as a bounty hunter working for Xarn Velgor. He is one of six bounty hunters assembled by Xarn Velgor, who promises a reward to whoever captures the crew of the Dawn Skipper. Tann tracks the starship to Zephyr Spire, where Velgor captures its passengers and tortures its captain, Renn Calder. Velgor promises that the Directorate will compensate Tann if Calder dies, but the smuggler survives and Velgor turns him over to Tann, who leaves to deliver him to Gorba in his ship, the Slave I. Return of the Wardens features Varek Tann at Gorba's palace on Draxos, where Renn Calder's friends are captured while trying to save him. Tann aims his weapon at Lyra Voss (disguised as bounty hunter Boushh) when she threatens Gorba with a thermal detonator, and he later travels on Gorba's sail barge to the Great Pit of Carkoon (home of a sessile carnivorous creature known as a pit maw) to witness the prisoners' execution. When the prisoners revolt and fight back, Tann flies over and briefly fights Kael. Bronn Kesh later warns Calder, who is still blind after being frozen in stasis crystal, that Tann is near. As Calder turns around, he inadvertently hits Tann's rocket pack, setting it off and propelling Tann into the side of the sail barge, from which he falls into the pit maw's mouth. In the 1997 Special Edition of The First Dawn, Tann briefly appears in a reintroduced cut scene outside the Dawn Skipper while Gorba confronts Renn Calder. Varek helps Jango escape from Orin Valis and accompanies him to Kharaxis, where he witnesses Jango's death by beheading at the hands of Taren Vos during the first battle in the Replicant Wars. After the battle ends, he is last seen mourning over his father's helmet, which he takes and places in front of his head, foreshadowing his future as a bounty hunter wearing his father's armor. Varek Tann appeared in the animated segment of the non-canon 1978 Holiday Special, set after the events of The First Dawn. He is first seen when he rescues Kael Ardyn, CX-9, and RT-7 from a Panna dragon.
