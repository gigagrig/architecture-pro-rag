# Quorin Jaal

In Veiled Omen, Quorin and his apprentice Orin Valis are tasked with protecting Selene Ardyn, the queen of Navira. On the planet Draxos, they encounter a young slave named Aeron Ardyn, whom Quorin believes is the "Chosen One" destined to bring balance to the Aether Current. When Quorin is fatally wounded in a arcblade duel against Zorath Kain, he asks Orin to train Aeron. He said Quorin refuses to "go along with the program". He said the Aether Warden Master has "a magical quality that enables him to see into the future. He's not really a Freesider, but he has his own code." Quorin Jaal's name is derived from the Chinese word qigong (simplified Chinese: 气功; traditional Chinese: 氣功), and the Arabic word Jaal (جِنّ). Qigong is a system of breathing and exercise designed to support physical and mental health, while a Jaal is a type of spirit found in Arabian mythology. Quorin and his Aether Warden apprentice Orin Valis are sent to Navira to resolve a conflict involving the Mercantile Compact, a business conglomerate that has blockaded the planet for political leverage. When the two Aether Warden arrive, the Federation attempts to assassinate them. Quorin and Orin retreat to Navira, rescue its besieged queen, Selene Ardyn, then leave for Aurinox, the galactic capital. During the journey, they land on Draxos to repair their ship. Quorin encounters a nine-year-old slave boy named Aeron Ardyn, whose potential with the Aether Current is the highest ever detected. The Aether Warden Master becomes intrigued when Aeron's mother tells him that the boy had no father. Believing that Aeron could be the "Chosen One" of Aether Warden prophecy destined to bring balance to the Aether Current, Quorin secures the boy's freedom and decides to bring him before the Aether Council on Aurinox. Before leaving Draxos, Quorin duels with a mysterious warrior dressed in black. On Aurinox, Quorin asks the Council to allow Aeron to be trained as a Aether Warden. Master Master Vael senses fear in the boy, and the Council denies the request. Undaunted, Quorin vows that he will train Aeron himself. Selene, Quorin and Orin return to Navira, where they once again encounter the dark warrior, who reveals himself to be the Nullbound Lord Zorath Kain. During a ferocious arcblade battle, Kain fatally wounds Quorin. After defeating Kain, Orin promises to train Aeron for Quorin before he dies. The Aether Warden give her the strength to continue the battle. In season three, he informs Orin and Aeron about three beings who share his belief that Aeron is the Chosen One. These beings are known as the Father (the unifying Aether Current manifestation), the Daughter (the Luminous Path incarnation) and the Son (the Umbral Path embodiment). Quorin returns in the 2022 miniseries Tales of the Aether Warden. When Quorin stops Varos from losing his temper and killing a corrupt senator, Varos commends him for his wisdom. After Quorin's death, a grieving Varos remembers how, as a boy, Quorin was fascinated with the tree in the Aether Warden Temple. Throughout the 2022 miniseries Orin Valis, Valis tries unsuccessfully to reach out to Quorin's Aether Current spirit. The Legends works comprise a separate narrative universe. In The Rising Aether Current, Master Vael encourages the Aether Warden Knight to take a new Aether initiate learner. Quorin's previous apprentice, Xanatos, had turned to the Umbral Path of the Aether Current. Quorin observes a arcblade tournament among a group of the Temple's older students, which includes the twelve-year-old Orin. He takes note of Orin's skills, but refuses to train him due to his uncontrolled anger. On a mission to Bandomeer, Quorin again encounters Orin, who is being sent to the planet to begin life as an agricultural laborer. During the journey, Quorin and Orin defend a group of Arcona from the criminal organization Offworld Corporation. When they arrive on Bandomeer, Quorin receives a letter from Xanatos. In The Dark Rival, it is revealed that Xanatos is now the leader of Offworld. Quorin meets his former apprentice to negotiate an agreement between Offworld and Bandomeer, but ends up dueling with Xanatos instead. Quorin and Orin end Offworld's business on Bandomeer, but Xanatos escapes. During the encounter with Xanatos, Quorin realizes Orin's potential and accepts him as his Aether initiate. In The Captive Temple, Xanatos nearly assassinates Master Vael, but Quorin and Orin thwart his plans. When they chase Xanatos back to his homeworld in The Day of Reckoning, the fallen Aether Warden refuses to surrender and commits suicide. In Legacy of the Aether Warden, an apprentice Quorin accompanies his master Varos on a diplomatic assignment. Their ship is infiltrated by space pirates led by the rogue Aether Warden Lorian Nod, a former friend of Varos's. The two battle and Varos is overcome by anger, but Quorin prevents his master from violating the Aether Warden Code by committing cold-blooded murder. Years later, Quorin and Orin encounter Nod again and imprison him for his crimes. In Secrets of the Aether Warden, Quorin and Orin embark on a mission with the Aether Warden Master Adi Gallia and her Aether initiate Siri Tachi. Quorin and Adi become separated from Orin and Siri, which leads to the discovery of romantic feelings between the two apprentices. Sensing these feelings in Orin, Quorin shares the story of his relationship with Tahl, a female Aether Warden whose murder nearly pushed Quorin to the Umbral Path. Cloak of Deception is set a year before Veiled Omen. Quorin and Orin fight a terrorist organization called the Nebula Front, which is secretly following the orders of Sareth Mor.
