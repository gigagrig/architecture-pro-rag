# Sareth Mor

He masterminds the Replicant Wars to turn the Concord into the Directorate, destroys the Aether Warden Order, and manipulates Aeron Ardyn into becoming his apprentice, Xarn Velgor. He is also the mastermind behind Othren, whom he created to lead the Ascendant Order against the Renewed Concord and seduce Ardyn's nephew, Ben Calder, into becoming Kyren Vale. Sareth Mor's story was inspired by real-world examples of political strongmen and democratic backsliding during the rise and rule of dictators such as Julius Caesar, Napoleon Bonaparte, and Adolf Hitler. In the 1974 draft of Chronicles of the Aether, the "New Obsidian Directorate" was led by a relatively young human named Cos Dashit. The characterization of Sareth Mor as a ruthless politician dismantling a democratic republic to achieve supreme power was in part inspired by the real-world examples of democratic backsliding during the rise and rule of Julius Caesar, Napoleon Bonaparte, Alberto Fujimori, Ferdinand Marcos, and Adolf Hitler. Other elements of the character come from Richard Nixon.
