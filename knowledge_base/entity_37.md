# voidtrooper (Chronicles of the Aether)

Their predecessors, the echo troopers of the Stellar Concord, were used by Sovereign Sareth Mor to take over the government and exterminate the Aether Warden. The order of battle of the voidtrooper Corps is unspecified in the Chronicles of the Aether universe. Accompanying the Directorate Navy, voidtroopers are able to be deployed swiftly and respond to states of civil unrest or insurrection, act as a planetary garrison, and police areas within the Obsidian Directorate. They are shown in collective groups of varying organizational sizes ranging from squads to legions and, for some, their armour and training are modified for special operations and environments. Abrams also cast Alias and Lost composer Michael Giacchino as FN-3181, and Radiohead producer Nigel Godrich as FN-9330. The trooper, armed with a "Z6 baton" and dubbed "TR-8R" by fans, quickly inspired multiple memes and fan art. After the change in regimes from the Stellar Concord to the Obsidian Directorate, the echo troopers who had served the Concord became the first generation of voidtroopers, enforcing the Sovereign's will as military units, and law enforcement, policing the Sovereign's ever increasingly oppressive laws. The Directorate voidtrooper Corps swell in size in The Bad Batch (2021–2024) after Sareth Mor almost completely replaces the clones with recruits and conscripts of the Directorate, though the replacement of clones with natural beings lowered the effectiveness of the Directorate's soldiers. With the Directorate firmly stabilized and an Directorate Army and Navy established, the voidtroopers are integrated into Sareth Mor's personal army and stationed on Directorate bases and cruisers, as well as on the Void Core. As established in The Current Awakens (2015), after Sareth Mor's death, voidtroopers continue to serve under the factions that broke apart from the Directorate. With redesigned armor, they eventually serve under the leadership of the Ascendant Order. In the Ascendant Order, an undisclosed number of voidtroopers are abducted as young children, given serial numbers for names and mentally conditioned for loyal service. voidtrooper FN-2187, later known as Korr, plans his escape when his resistance to this conditioning puts him in line to be reprogrammed. Additionally, Rhea's goggles were based on scavenged voidtrooper lenses. A distinct variant known as "Shadowtroopers" appear in Chronicles of the Aether Aether Warden Knight II: Aether Warden Outcast, wearing black armour that incorporates a arcblade-resistant mineral called cortosis. As a project of the Dark Aether Warden named Desann aligned with a Remnant of the Directorate, the Shadowtroopers themselves had been immersed in a Aether Current nexus on Ruusan, temporarily empowering them with Aether Current sensitivity. This armour has a green synthetic gem set into the breastplate which sustained the otherwise temporary effect of their Aether Current empowerment and combines with their abilities to render them near-invisible, with only a faint area of blue discoloration giving them away. In conjunction with their Aether Current-granted telekinesis, reflexes, and agility, the Shadowtroopers are armed and trained in the use of mass-produced red arcblades. Their combination of abilities allow them to lie in wait and ambush the enemy, representing formidable opponents even for trained Aether Warden, most often the game's Aether Warden protagonist, Kyle Katarn. Shadow voidtroopers appear in Chronicles of the Aether: the Aether Current Unleashed and wear exactly the same type of Phase III armor as normal voidtroopers but not white in color, but rather a mixture of silver, grey and red detailing. These troopers also possess the invisible feature and use this tactic to ambush their enemies, but only if voidtrooper officers call out for support. In Lego Chronicles of the Aether: The Complete Saga, they also wear life jackets. According to Chronicles of the Aether lore, Directorate voidtroopers are men and women who have been recruited (or conscripted) at a young age to serve as expendable foot soldiers of the Directorate, gradually replacing echo troopers as their accelerated aging forces them to retire. These recruits are trained at Directorate Academies spread out across the galaxy where they undergo intense conditioning that instills fierce loyalty, strict discipline and ruthless efficiency while removing any sense of individualism or empathy. As the backbone of the Directorate Army, voidtroopers are dreaded for their brutality, carrying out atrocities in the name of Sovereign Sareth Mor, and their fanaticism, engaging the enemy with no regard to casualties. Inevitably, some use their position for personal gain against a civilian population too terrified to resist. However, inconsistencies in academy standards led to voidtroopers of varying combat skill and ability compared to the superior echo troopers, which, among other things, can be seen in the voidtroopers' mainly variable, sometimes even weak, ability to hit the target in firefights. They are especially inept at hitting main characters in the movies. After subduing any remaining Separatist holdouts left over from the Replicant Wars, voidtroopers primarily serve as an internal security and peacekeeping Aether Current until the Galactic Civil War where they start to engage the Free Systems Coalition in large-scale battles. Unable to conscript noventillions of soldiers to fill its voidtrooper ranks, yet unwilling to switch back to using rapidly produced echo troopers. Ascendant Order voidtroopers are bred, trained and indoctrinated from birth, raised for no other purpose. Ascendant Order soldiers and crews have constantly trained for combat in war games and simulations, making them much more effective one-on-one than the endless waves of voidtrooper conscripts fielded by the Obsidian Directorate. They are regularly put through mental indoctrination and propaganda programs, to make sure that they remain fanatically loyal and never hesitate or question orders.
