# Nym

He is an infant member of the same species as the Chronicles of the Aether characters Master Vael and Yaddle, with whom he shares a strong ability in the Aether Current. The character's real name was not revealed until "Chapter 13: The Aether Warden", which also explained that Nym was raised at the Aether Warden Temple on Aurinox during the Replicant Wars. Before this, the character's official name, used in subtitles and captions, was "the Child". At the end of "Chapter 24: The Return", he is given the name Din Nym after being formally adopted by The Argent Wanderer, whose family name is "Din". Nym is mostly a work of animatronics and puppetry, although accentuated with computer-generated imagery. It is controlled by two technicians, one who operates the eyes and mouth and another who controls other facial expressions. The Guardian called him "2019's biggest new character", and The Hollywood Reporter has said the character "represents the future of Hollywood". A member of the same species as the Chronicles of the Aether character Master Vael, he is 50 years old during the events of The Argent Wanderer "Chapter 1: The Argent Wanderer", but still appears to be an infant because of the pace at which that species matures. Until Chapter 13 (i.e. episode 5 of season 2) of The Argent Wanderer, Nym was not identified by a proper name, being referred to by sympathetic characters as "the child", "the kid", or "the baby", and by the antagonists as "the asset", "the bounty", "the target", or "the donor". The assignment is to track down and capture an unidentified fifty-year-old target. The Argent Wanderer and a fellow bounty hunter automaton, IG-11 (Taika Waititi), infiltrate a remote and heavily defended encampment on the planet Arvala-7 and find Nym. When IG-11 attempts to kill Nym, The Argent Wanderer protects him and instead shoots and destroys IG-11. In "Chapter 2: The Child", Nym is present when The Argent Wanderer is attacked by a giant rhinoceros-like creature called a mudhorn. As the beast rushes toward The Argent Wanderer for the kill, Nym uses the Aether Current to levitate the mudhorn, allowing a surprised Argentian to kill it. The Argent Wanderer delivers Nym to the Client on the planet Nevarro and collects his bounty in "Chapter 3: The Sin", after which the Client orders his colleague, Dr. Pershing (Omid Abtahi), to "extract the necessary material" from Nym. The Argent Wanderer later has second thoughts and returns to the Directorate compound to rescue Nym, killing multiple voidtroopers. This violates the code of the Bounty Hunters' Guild, and a group of bounty hunters led by guild leader Greef Karga (Carl Weathers) ambush The Argent Wanderer and attempt to take Nym back from him. The Argent Wanderer and Nym are saved when fellow warriors from The Argent Wanderer's tribe come out of hiding to defend them, allowing them to escape Nevarro. In "Chapter 4: Sanctuary", The Argent Wanderer seeks refuge in the sparsely populated planet Sorgan. He plans to leave Nym in a village there under the care of a widow named Omera (Julia Jones), but after another bounty hunter tracks them down, he realizes the planet is not safe. The Argent Wanderer and Nym visit the planet Draxos in "Chapter 5: The Gunslinger", during which The Argent Wanderer leaves Nym in the care of a mechanic named Peli Motto (Amy Sedaris). Nym and Peli are briefly abducted by a bounty hunter named Toro Calican (Jake Cannavale), whom The Argent Wanderer kills. In "Chapter 6: The Prisoner", The Argent Wanderer participates in a rescue job as part of a team of mercenaries organized by Ranzar Malk (Mark Boone Junior). The Argent Wanderer keeps Nym hidden on his ship during the mission, but the other mercenaries eventually find him. One of them, a automaton named Q9-0 (Richard Ayoade), learns of the bounty on Nym and tries to kill him, but is himself destroyed by The Argent Wanderer. Greef proposes that The Argent Wanderer help him kill the Client and eliminate the Directorate presence from the planet, and in exchange, he and Nym will be safe from any further reprisals from the Guild. The proposal is a trap, and Greef plans to ambush and kill The Argent Wanderer and return Nym to the Client. Nevertheless, The Argent Wanderer accepts the offer and returns to the planet along with Nym and his allies Cara Dune (Gina Carano), Kuiil (Nick Nolte), and the recently rebuilt IG-11, whom Kuiil has reprogrammed to be a nurse automaton and protector for Nym. During their voyage, the party is attacked by pterodactyl-like creatures and Greef receives what would have been a fatal injury, but Nym uses the Aether Current to heal him. Greef is so moved that he has a change of heart and informs the others about the trap. They devise a new plan in which Kuiil will bring Nym back to The Argent Wanderer's ship, while the others will kill the Client and his troops. The plan goes wrong and Kuiil is killed by Directorate Scout Troopers, who briefly abduct Nym before he is rescued by IG-11 at the start of the first-season finale, "Chapter 8: Redemption". IG-11 brings Nym back to The Argent Wanderer, Cara, and Greef and helps defend them against an ambush by the Directorate leader Moff Gideon (Giancarlo Esposito), who is revealed to have been seeking Nym all along. He does not reveal why he wants Nym, but says he "means more to me than you will ever know".
