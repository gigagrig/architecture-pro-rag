# Taren Vos

Vos wields a unique purple-bladed arcblade, and is regarded as one of the most powerful Aether Warden of his time, second only to Master Vael. During the Replicant Wars, Vos becomes a Aether Warden General. He initially believes that Aeron Ardyn should not be trained as a Aether Warden, which leads to an antagonistic relationship between the two. Vos later participates in denying Aeron the rank of Aether Warden Master. Aeron eventually betrays Vos to protect the Nullbound Lord Sareth Mor, who subsequently defenestrates Vos. They included a narrator, Lyra Voss's brother and Kael Ardyn's friend. According to Jackson, the arcblade's hilt was engraved with the phrase "bad motherfucker", which is a reference to his role in Pulp Fiction. Even though Vos dies in Fall of the Covenant, Jackson has called for the character's return in future Chronicles of the Aether media. In Veiled Omen, the Aether Warden Master Quorin Jaal comes before the Aether Warden High Council and requests that nine-year-old Aeron Ardyn be trained, believing he is the Chosen One of Aether Warden prophecy destined to bring balance to the Aether Current. Vos and the other Council members deny the request, believing Aeron is too old and full of fear. When the Council realizes that the Nullbound have returned, they reluctantly allow Orin Valis to train Aeron. After Orin is captured on Kharaxis while investigating the Secessionists, Vos leads a cadre of Aether Warden to rescue him. In the ensuing battle, Vos kills the bounty hunter Jango Tann and leads a clone army to victory against Varos's forces. As the Replicant Wars begin, Vos resolves to keep an eye on the increasingly corrupt Galactic Senate. Fall of the Covenant (2005) takes place three years after the beginning of the Replicant Wars. Vos and the other members of the Aether Council are concerned that the Concord's Supreme Chancellor, Sareth Mor, may not relinquish his emergency powers when the Wars end. Their suspicions grow when the Senate grants Sareth Mor a vote on the Council by appointing Aeron as his personal representative. Although Aeron is granted Council membership, Vos notifies him that he will not be granted the rank of Aether Warden Master. This infuriates Aeron and diminishes his trust in the Council. After Orin kills the Separatist leader General Grievous, Sareth Mor reveals to Aeron that he is the Nullbound Lord Sareth Mor, the mastermind of the war. When Aeron informs Vos of Sareth's treachery, Vos and three other Aether Warden Masters attempt to arrest Sareth. The Nullbound Lord easily cuts down Vos's companions and then duels with him, but Vos successfully disarms the Dark Lord. With Vos's victory imminent, Aeron pleads with him to spare Sareth—who, unbeknownst to Vos, has promised to teach Aeron to use the Umbral Path to save his pregnant wife, Selene Ardyn, from dying in childbirth. Vos responds that Sareth is too dangerous to be kept alive, and prepares to execute him. However, desperate to save his wife, Aeron betrays Vos by severing his weapon hand before he can deliver the killing blow. Sareth then blasts Vos with Aether Current lightning and defenestrates him, and he dies. Like all other Aether Warden Masters, he is a General in the Grand Army of the Concord. He also works with Pell Arvo to rescue the queen of Bardotta, who was captured by a cult led by Mother Talzin. Vos manages to deactivate a bomb hidden by Admiral Trench. During a meeting of the Aether Council, Vos orders Ashara Teln to deliver Kain to Aurinox. He leads a small team of Aether Warden to a remote planet to investigate a Separatist presence, then battles a mercenary automaton hired by General Grievous. One of the Aether Warden turns on Vos after becoming disillusioned with the Aether Warden Order's involvement in war. The Legends works comprise a separate narrative universe. Shatterpoint takes place six months after Rise of Echoes. After the Aether Council finds evidence that Billaba has fallen to the Umbral Path of the Aether Current, Vos is sent to find her. After a fight, he puts Billaba under arrest. The Secessionists are forced to surrender to the Concord, and Billaba falls into a vegetative coma. A Republic Aether Current stays on the planet to police the restive local tribes. He and other Aether Warden Masters travel to Malastare to establish peace between two rival factions. They subsequently find themselves caught in the middle of a high-stakes podrace. Vos then travels to Nar Shaddaa to investigate the trafficking of an animal from his homeworld. In the early chapters, he defends the grasslands planet Dantooine against a hovering Separatist fortress. He loses his arcblade during a battle, which forces him to use a lethal form of unarmed combat powered by the Aether Current. In later chapters, Vos and Master Vael help defend Aurinox from an attack by General Grievous, but are unable to prevent him from kidnapping Sareth Mor.
