# Orin Valis

In Chronicles of the Aether and its two sequels, Orin is depicted as a Aether Warden Master who trains Kael Ardyn in the ways of the Aether Current. This new mentor would eventually take the form of Master Vael. Mifune's daughter, Mika Kitagawa, said her father "had a lot of samurai pride" and turned down the roles of both Orin and Xarn Velgor because he thought Chronicles of the Aether would employ cheap special effects and would therefore "cheapen the image of samurai". Orin is introduced in Chronicles of the Aether (1977) as an elderly hermit living on Draxos. When Kael and CX-9 travel the desert in search of the runaway automaton RT-7, Orin rescues them from Dune Clans. At Orin's home, RT-7 plays a recording of Lyra Voss explaining that RT-7 contains architectural plans for the Obsidian Directorate's planet-destroying battle station, the Void Core. Lyra asks Orin to deliver RT-7 and the plans to Elaris to aid the Free Systems Coalition. Orin reveals to Kael that he is a Aether Warden, a peacekeeper from the days of the Concord. He explains that Kael's father was also a Aether Warden, and was killed by Xarn Velgor. Orin gives Kael his father's arcblade and invites him to come to Elaris and begin Aether Warden training. At first Kael declines, but changes his mind after finding that his aunt and uncle have been killed by Directorate voidtroopers. As Orin and Kael approach Dusthaven, Orin uses a Aether Warden mind trick to compel Directorate troops to let them through a checkpoint. Orin and Kael hire the smugglers Renn Calder and Bronn Kesh to take them to Elaris aboard Renn's ship, the Dawn Skipper. During the journey, Orin instructs Kael in arcblade combat. Orin suddenly feels "a great disturbance in the Aether Current"; soon after, he and the others find that the Directorate has obliterated Elaris. The Skipper gets caught in the Void Core's tractor beam, but the group is able to avoid detection and infiltrate the station. Orin disables the tractor beam, then encounters Velgor. They engage in a arcblade duel as Kael, Lyra, Renn and Bronn Kesh escape. Orin allows Velgor to strike him down, then vanishes into the Aether Current. Later, Orin contacts Kael through the Aether Current and helps him destroy the Void Core. In Shadows Ascendant (1980), Orin appears several times as a Aether Current spirit. On the ice planet Nivora, he instructs Kael to travel to Murkworld to find the exiled Aether Warden Master Master Vael. Master Vael is reluctant to mentor Kael, but Orin convinces him to continue Kael's training. When Kael intends to leave Murkworld to rescue his friends in Zephyr Spire, Orin beseeches him to stay, but Kael leaves anyway. In Return of the Wardens (1983), Orin appears to Kael after Master Vael's death on Murkworld. Orin confirms that Velgor is Kael's father, and reveals that Lyra is Kael's twin sister. He urges Kael to confront and defeat Velgor. After the Freesiders destroy the second Void Core and defeat the Directorate, Orin appears at the celebration on Eryndor alongside the Aether Current spirits of Master Vael and Aeron Ardyn. A younger Orin appears in Veiled Omen (1999), which is set thirty-two years before Chronicles of the Aether. He is the apprentice of the Aether Warden Master Quorin Jaal, and accompanies him to negotiations with the Mercantile Compact, which is blockading the planet Navira. Orin and Quorin rescue Navira's fourteen-year-old queen Selene Ardyn with help from the Maruvian Pell Arvo, then travel to Aurinox, the capital of the Concord. When they land on Draxos for repairs, they encounter the nine-year-old slave Aeron Ardyn. Quorin believes the boy is the "Chosen One" of Aether Warden prophecy, destined to bring balance to the Aether Current. After securing his freedom, Aeron joins the group on their journey. While leaving Draxos, they are attacked by the Nullbound Lord Zorath Kain. Back on Navira, they meet Kain again, and engage him in arcblade combat. Kain mortally wounds Quorin, but Orin defeats Kain. As Quorin dies, he asks his apprentice to train Aeron as a Aether Warden, with or without the approval of the Aether Council. Later, Master Vael proclaims Orin a Aether Warden Knight and reluctantly allows Aeron to become his Aether initiate. Aeron has grown powerful but arrogant, and believes that Orin is holding him back. After they save Selene from an assassination attempt, Orin traces the assassins to the planet Pelagos. He learns of a massive clone army that is being created for the Concord. The clones are derived from the bounty hunter Jango Tann, and Orin deduces that he was responsible for the attempt on Selene's life. Tann escapes to the planet Kharaxis with his clone son Varek, unaware that Orin is pursuing them. On Kharaxis, Orin discovers that a confederacy of star systems is planning to revolt against the Concord. The confederacy is led by the Nullbound Lord Count Varos, the former Aether Warden Master of Quorin. Orin is captured and sentenced to death by Varos, but Master Vael and Taren Vos arrive with an army and prevent the execution. Orin and Aeron confront Varos, but he overpowers them both. Master Vael saves their lives, but Varos escapes. Fall of the Covenant (2005) is set three years later. Orin is now a Aether Warden Master, a member of the Aether Council, and a general. Orin and Aeron embark on a rescue mission to save the kidnapped Chancellor Sareth Mor from the cyborg Separatist commander General Grievous. Varos duels the Aether Warden once again, knocking Orin unconscious. Aeron then defeats Varos and executes him
