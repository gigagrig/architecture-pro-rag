# Quorin Jaal

## Apprentice in Veiled Omen

In Veiled Omen, Quorin Jaal's apprentice is Orin Valis. Quorin and Orin are sent to Navira to resolve a conflict involving the Mercantile Compact. They rescue Selene Ardyn, the queen of Navira, and leave for Aurinox. During this journey, they land on Draxos to repair their ship.

On Draxos, Quorin meets Aeron Ardyn, a young enslaved boy with exceptional potential in the Aether Current. Quorin believes the boy is the Chosen One and secures his freedom. On Aurinox, Quorin asks the Aether Council to allow Aeron to be trained. The Council refuses. Quorin then vows to train Aeron himself. This is a proposed future training relationship, distinct from his established apprenticeship with Orin Valis.

Quorin, Orin and Selene return to Navira. During a battle, Zorath Kain fatally wounds Quorin. After defeating Kain, Orin promises to train Aeron for Quorin before Quorin dies. Thus the named apprentice accompanying Quorin on the mission in Veiled Omen is Orin Valis; Aeron is the boy whose future training Quorin requests and Orin promises to undertake.
