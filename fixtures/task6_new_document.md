# Lumen Relay Observatory

The Lumen Relay Observatory is an isolated navigation station in the fictional QuantumForge knowledge universe. Its maintenance team uses the amber beacon protocol to synchronize navigation clocks. The protocol requires operators to compare three independent timing signals before opening the docking corridor. A mismatch closes the corridor and creates a maintenance ticket for the next watch. The station coordinator is Mira Solven, who reviews the ticket and records the repair in the observatory journal. Visiting crews receive a temporary docking code after all signals agree. The observatory stores daily calibration reports locally and copies approved reports to the central archive. Emergency drills take place every ninth day. During a drill, crews use a separate practice corridor and do not interrupt routine calibration work.
